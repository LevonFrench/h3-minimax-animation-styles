# Research — Early Ink-Line Animation

## Actionable vocabulary

- Early drawn animation grew from thousands of separate line drawings on paper; registration systems kept images aligned while hand redraw introduced natural boil.
- India-ink contours on translucent paper, sparse backgrounds, frame flicker, and slight line-density variation evoke the period better than modern distress overlays.
- Centered abstract transformation should be drawn frame by frame: breathing contour, double-line jitter, orbiting hatch marks, and return to a clean circle.
- Keep camera and paper registration fixed; the lively instability belongs inside the ink line.
