# Title

Ink Circle Breathing Study

## Intent

Animate lively instability inside one registered ink contour while the paper and camera remain absolutely fixed.

## Shot Class

Centered top-down paper close-up, locked animation-stand camera.

## Subject / Continuity

One thin India-ink circle on translucent off-white paper. It remains a circle throughout; registration, center, average diameter, and paper field do not drift.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): clean circle. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): contour breathes outward with slight hand-redrawn boil and occasional double-line jitter. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): six short hatch marks orbit clockwise around it while circle contracts. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): hatch marks rejoin the contour and line density evens. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): clean opening circle returns. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Circle centered at (0,0,0), average diameter 0.26 m, variation ≤0.025 m. Camera fixed at (0,+0.65,0).

## Look + Lighting

Early hand-drawn India ink, translucent paper, sparse field, natural line boil, slight frame flicker and density variation, no digital distress overlay.

## Sound

Silent; no projector noise, music, voice, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One breathing contour cycle. No camera drift, paper movement, object metamorphosis, added color, cuts, text, logos, or motion after 4.80 s.
