# Research — Rough animation pencil test: flight cycles and landing

## Provenance and identification

This is a rough pencil-test presentation of cyclical flight and landing. Disney identifies rough posing, timing, weight, overlapping action, cleanup, and in-betweens as parts of hand-drawn production. The Academy guide supports frame-by-frame planning. Onion-skin ghosts and colored construction/correction lines are useful production visualization, but not proof of one studio-specific pipeline.

## Visual mechanics

- Coordinate two wing cycles with a shallow rise-and-fall body arc.
- Cluster drawings at top and bottom reversals; widen them through fastest mid-strokes.
- Stabilize the head, delay tail counter-arcs, and tuck feet on smaller paths.
- Convert flight momentum into branch contact and one compact body-settle arc.

## Prompt implications

Count exactly two readable cycles and one landing. Keep the branch and page registration fixed; avoid ghosting that produces extra birds.
