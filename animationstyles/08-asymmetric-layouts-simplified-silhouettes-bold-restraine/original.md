# Animation style 8

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 limited animation composed for 864x480, using asymmetric layouts, simplified silhouettes, bold restrained color blocks, sparse linework, crisp poster-like staging, and economical held poses. An angular office worker stands beside an enormous red filing cabinet against a cream-and-teal background. His body remains in a held pose while only his eyes follow a drawer sliding open by itself. He rotates one wrist through two clean key drawings, pushes the drawer closed, and resumes the original pose. Every other drawer then opens in a precise graphic rhythm as his pupils shift left and right without moving his head.

## Soundscape

Each drawer produces a clean wooden slide and dry stop, accompanied by a quiet office ventilation tone and one restrained paper rustle.
