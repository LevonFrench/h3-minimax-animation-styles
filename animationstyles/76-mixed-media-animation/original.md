# Animation style 76

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 mixed-media animation composed for 864x480, combining layered paper, felt texture, metallic foil highlights, painted accents, and stop-motion-style assembly. A single sphere remains perfectly centered at constant size with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere begins as matte paper, then transitions to fuzzy felt, then reflective foil, then brushed paint, each medium wrapping the same centered spherical form without changing its overall scale. The materials peel, fold, and overlay in clearly readable stages. During the final second, all four material treatments retract and recombine into the exact original paper sphere, restoring the opening composition for montage consistency.

## Soundscape

Paper folds, felt brushing, foil crinkles, and wet paint strokes create a layered tactile sound texture.

## Music

A handcrafted mixed ensemble of toy piano, soft percussion, plucked strings, and airy synth pads accompanies the material changes with a gentle rhythmic flow.
