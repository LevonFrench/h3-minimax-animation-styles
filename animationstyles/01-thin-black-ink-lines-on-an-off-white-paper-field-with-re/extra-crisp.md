# Title

The Cartographer's Impossible Coast

## Intent

Make economical ink drawing feel alive through contour inheritance: one unbroken coastline redraws itself into a sleeping sea serpent, with every intermediate state visibly made from the same line.

## Shot Class

Top-down medium insert, 50 mm equivalent, locked rostrum camera, perpendicular to paper.

## Subject / Continuity

Off-white toothy paper and one thin black-ink contour. The line remains materially identical throughout; no visible hand, pen, text, or extra marks.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM; one beat = 0.600 s. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): irregular coastline rests. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): its eastern inlet curls inward as a continuous pen-like stroke. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): inherited bays become the serpent's jaw and eye without dissolving. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): the western shore lifts into one arched back, uneven redraws producing restrained line boil. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): the final tail closes the silhouette. Hold the resolved sleeping serpent and locked camera from 4.80-5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Drawing occupies the XY paper plane, centered at (0,0,0), with contour endpoints moving no farther than 0.18 m; camera fixed at (0,+0.65,0), aimed straight down, no roll or reframing.

## Look + Lighting

Early hand-drawn animation: thin variable ink, slight registration weave, off-white fibers, visible shared-contour metamorphosis. Even warm copy-table light stays constant.

## Sound

Silent visual take; no dialogue, music, effects, or generated soundtrack.

## Limits

One continuous take and one transformation action. No cuts, fades, morph blur, color, shading, typography, hand, pen, new disconnected lines, camera drift, or perfect digital tweening.
