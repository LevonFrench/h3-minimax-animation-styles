# Research — Stylized CG sequential overlap

## Provenance and identification

This is contemporary stylized character CG organized around a sequential turn, not a documented named style. The reliable vocabulary is anticipation, weight, overlapping action, follow-through, and spline-shaped spacing. Disney describes those principles in character animation; Blender documents F-curves as the mechanism for controlling interpolated motion and materials as the basis of light response.

## Visual mechanics

- Stage a causal rotation chain: eyes lead, then head, neck, shoulders, torso, hips.
- Treat ears and heavy tail as separate inertial masses with delayed arcs and decreasing oscillations.
- Hold the feet and ground contact stable so the turn reads as rotation through one body, not sliding pieces.
- Use soft PBR lighting and a clear desert silhouette; avoid uniform interpolation across every joint.

## Prompt implications

Name the order and overlaps explicitly. The “same” in the source is comparative wording, not an independent historical style claim. The prompt should make the final backward-facing pose arrive before the ear and tail settle.
