# Animation style 4

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 animator's pencil test composed for 864x480, showing rough graphite construction lines, blue underdrawing, red correction marks, registration holes, exposed timing notes, and fluctuating line density. An original young acrobat prepares for a standing leap. She lowers through a strong anticipation pose, compresses her knees, drives upward through a clean line of action, and rotates once. Closely spaced drawings ease her into the apex; wider spacing accelerates the descent. Her arms follow curved arcs, loose hair overlaps one beat late, and she lands with visible squash before settling into a balanced key pose.

## Soundscape

Graphite scratches, paper sheets flip rapidly, shoes make a soft landing thump, and fabric moves faintly during the tum.

## Music

A metronomic piano pattern marks the preparation, leap, apex, and landing without obscuring the animation timing.
