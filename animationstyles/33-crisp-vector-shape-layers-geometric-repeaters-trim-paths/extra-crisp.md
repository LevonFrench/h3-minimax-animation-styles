# Title

Hexagon Star Phase

## Intent

Run one diagonal phase wave through a tessellation with integer cycle counts and an exact repeated state before the final H3 hold.

## Shot Class

Full-frame orthographic vector tile, locked camera.

## Subject / Continuity

Seamless teal hexagon repeaters tile beyond every edge; their negative spaces form saffron six-point stars. All cells have identical geometry and edge wrap.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): phase zero. Beats 2-7 (0.60-4.20): one diagonal wave crosses bottom-left to top-right as each hexagon performs exactly one 60-degree rotation at constant rate; negative-space stars exchange size without easing. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): every cell returns to the identical opening phase. Hold the exact recurrence through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Repeater grid fills X=±0.70, Y=±0.40 on Z=0 with seamless edge wrap. Camera fixed at (0,0,−1.5).

## Look + Lighting

Crisp parametric shape layers, whole-number rotations, exact tessellation, negative-space stars, flat teal/saffron palette, constant motion-blur setting.

## Sound

Silent; no music, effects, voice, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One phase-wave action. No fades, variable easing, scale drift, broken edge wrap, camera movement, text, logos, or changes during final hold. Treat loop seam as post-verified.
