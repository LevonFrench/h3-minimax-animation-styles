# Title

Amber Ball Gravity Study

## Intent

Demonstrate gravity with clean cel extremes, volume-preserving squash and stretch, and a round held endpoint.

## Shot Class

Medium-wide side view, 55 mm equivalent, locked camera.

## Subject / Continuity

One amber rubber ball with confident ink contour bounces on a stable wooden floor. Apparent volume, color, floor line, and cast shadow remain consistent.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): ball holds near apex. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): descent spacing widens; ball elongates vertically along velocity. Beat 4 (1.80-2.40): one compressed impact spreads horizontally. Beats 5-6 (2.40-3.60): lower rebound rises with close spacing near apex. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): ball descends and makes a smaller settling contact. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): it rolls 0.10 m and becomes perfectly round. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Ball X=−0.10 to +0.10 m, Y=0-0.48 m; floor Y=0. Camera fixed at (0,+0.25,−1.8).

## Look + Lighting

Classical ink-and-paint cel animation, opaque amber fill, clean in-betweens, warm theatrical floor, stable key and shadow.

## Sound

Silent; no bounce foley, dialogue, music, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One diminishing bounce action. No extra balls, changing volume, decorative camera motion, floor warp, cuts, text, logos, or motion after 4.80 s.
