# Research — Stop-Motion Hesitation and Delayed Prop Motion

## Actionable vocabulary

- Stage a single decision: reach → withdraw/listen → shield with parcel → backward anticipation → commit through the door.
- Preserve planted feet during the listening lean so the silhouette communicates reluctance. The parcel should lag the torso by one pose as overlapping action.
- Replacement facial pieces should change sparingly at held extremes; excessive face swapping causes identity drift.
- Keep the dark doorway and hinged door spatially stable. One practical interior sound motivates the commitment.
