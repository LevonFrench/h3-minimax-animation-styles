# Animation style 64

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 animation composed for 864x480, using xerographic cel technique with rough transferred pencil contours, dry-brush energy, flatter painted color, and visible line texture. A single orange sphere remains perfectly centered on a pale neutral field at a constant size with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere jolts into a quick spin, smears into a horizontal streak for one frame, snaps back into a circle, and then wobbles through two settling oscillations. The rough outline darkens and lightens as if photocopied through successive cels, before returning to a stable centered sphere.

## Soundscape

Fast swishes, one brief smear-like zip, and two soft wobble taps define the rougher cel energy.
