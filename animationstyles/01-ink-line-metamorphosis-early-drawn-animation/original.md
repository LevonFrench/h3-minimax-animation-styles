# Animation style 1

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 animation composed for 864x480, created from thin black ink lines on an off-white paper field with restless line boil, sparse staging, faint frame flicker, and early straight-ahead animation. An unseen pen draws a small bird one continuous stroke at a time. The bird flaps through uneven but readable arcs, pecks the ground, and pulls the horizon line upward. Its beak stretches into a windmill blade, its body becomes the mill house, and the remaining lines curl into clouds. Every transformation passes through visible intermediate shapes rather than dissolving.

## Soundscape

Pen nibs scratch across paper, wings produce delicate paper-like flutters, and the completed windmill creaks softly beneath a faint mechanical projector rattle.

## Music

A lively solo upright-piano accompaniment uses short ascending runs and playful rhythmic accents, ending with one bright chord.
