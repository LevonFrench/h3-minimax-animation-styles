# Title

The Clockmaker Sets the Comet

## Intent

Show tactile puppet weight through planted feet, compressed fabric, stepped increments, and one motivated facial replacement.

## Shot Class

Medium full shot, 60 mm macro equivalent, locked miniature-stage camera at puppet eye level.

## Subject / Continuity

A carved-wood clockmaker puppet in a moss-green wool coat places one brass comet into a tabletop orrery. Keep face plates, button spacing, wood grain, and practical contact shadows consistent.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): puppet braces with both feet planted. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): torso leans and coat fabric compresses as the comet rises in both hands. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): hands carry it along one stepped arc toward the orrery socket. Beat 6 (3.00-3.60): comet seats with a tiny contact jolt. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): one replacement mouth changes from strain to relief. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): puppet settles, hands resting beside the mechanism. Hold through 5.1667 s; camera locked.

## Spatial Block

Puppet remains at (0,0,0); comet moves from (−0.12,+0.16,−0.02) to (+0.10,+0.23,+0.08). Camera fixed at (0,+0.20,−1.25), 60 mm.

## Look + Lighting

Carved wood, visible wool weave, metal armature suggestion, stepped pose increments, slight registration flutter, practical miniature shadows. Warm work-lamp key subtly intensifies when the comet seats.

## Sound

Silent take; no speech, music, foley, or generated soundtrack.

## Limits

One placement action. No suitcase, railway, lifting walk, fluid CG interpolation, floating feet, changing wardrobe, duplicate props, cuts, camera motion, text, logos, or endpoint jitter.
