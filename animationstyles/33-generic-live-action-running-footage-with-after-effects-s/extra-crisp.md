# Title

Sunrise Climber Track

## Intent

Integrate one restrained graphic cue into live action with rigid ground-plane and torso tracking.

## Shot Class

Medium-wide live-action side track, 50 mm equivalent, continuous gimbal path parallel to subject.

## Subject / Continuity

One climber in a fixed blue windbreaker hikes uphill at sunrise. A thin coral elevation path stays attached to the ground; one small circular effort gauge stays rigidly attached beside the torso.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): left boot plants. Beats 2-6 (0.60-3.60): climber takes one continuous uphill stride cycle while camera tracks parallel; coral path draws behind the planted foot and perspective-scales with terrain. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): torso gauge fills once as right boot lands. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): climber pauses in profile and overlays hold perfectly attached. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Climber (−0.55,0,0) to (+0.45,+0.18,+0.12); camera (−0.50,+1.25,−3.0) to (+0.50,+1.43,−2.88), matched parallel path.

## Look + Lighting

Natural sunrise live action, stable planar tracking, corner-pin perspective, restrained coral vector overlay, realistic scale change. Warm rim grows subtly as subject crests.

## Sound

Silent; no breathing, dialogue, music, footsteps, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One climb cycle. No runner, pace text, sliding overlays, jitter, extra people, cuts, camera orbit, logos, readable metrics, or endpoint motion.
