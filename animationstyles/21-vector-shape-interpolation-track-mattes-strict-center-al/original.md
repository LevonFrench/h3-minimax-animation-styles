# Animation style 21

## Prompt

A5.00-second 16:9 graphic-advertising animation composed for 864x480, using vector shape interpolation, track mattes, strict center alignment, smooth color-region exchange, and clean elastic easing. A yellow circle becomes a simplified sun, its rays retract, and its central shape elongates into a light bulb. The bulb's glass narrows into a water droplet, which splashes outward and reforms as a green leaf. The leat folds along its center and becomes a check mark inside a rounded square. Each icon holds for a brief readable beat before transforming through continuous intermediate silhouettes. The final card reads "SMALL CHANGES. CLEAR RESULTS." in bold black type beneath the green check mark.

## Soundscape

Soft tonal pops identify each icon, while the droplet produces one clean splash and the final check mark lands with a compact interface click.

## Music

Bright electronic mallets, restrained bass pulses, and soft hand percussion follow the four-stage morphing rhythm.
