# Research — Silhouette cutout animation

## Provenance and identification

The Academy's animation guide distinguishes silhouette animation—cut shapes photographed against backlight—from collage arranged and shot from above, and lists paper among frame-by-frame materials. The source combines that established technique with an ornate theatrical-artifact treatment: black paper, amber illumination, hinged flaps, and decorative stars. “Ornate” is direction, not a separate documented process.

## Visual mechanics

- Preserve opaque black shapes and a high-contrast amber backlight.
- Articulate each paper flap around a visible implied hinge in stepped increments.
- Keep the central circle registered while stars move radially and return.
- Let paper fibers and tiny edge irregularities remain visible without turning into fuzzy volumetric fur.

## Prompt implications

Count four flaps, use symmetrical staging, and require every star and flap to recombine into the pristine circle before the hold.
