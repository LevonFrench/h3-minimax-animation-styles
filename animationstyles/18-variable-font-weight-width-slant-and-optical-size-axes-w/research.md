# Research — Variable-font axis animation

## Identification and provenance

The prompt combines axis animation with word-to-word morphing; sources document variable axes, while cross-word glyph correspondence is an authored hybrid technique.

## Historically and technically grounded mechanics

Registered weight, width, slant, and optical-size axes interpolate continuously while counters, stems, and glyph recognition remain intact. A responsive frame can track changing text metrics.

## Actionable motion, material, and camera vocabulary

- Preserve material evidence, edge behavior, motivated overlap, readable spacing, and a stable endpoint.
- Treat axis changes as typographic interpolation rather than rubber distortion; preserve letter identity and align the exact final word stack to one left grid.
- Camera vocabulary: locked orthographic layout view, zero amplitude, zero speed; continuous screen direction; no unmotivated reframing.

## Prompt implications

Name visible causes, not style alone. Preserve the original subject/action, use legible intermediate states and coherent space, and reserve the final tail for a settled hold.
