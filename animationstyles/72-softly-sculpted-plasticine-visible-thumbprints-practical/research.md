# Research — Plasticine Metamorphosis

## Actionable vocabulary

- Clay animation should retain plasticine fingerprints, finger dents, tool marks, tacky pull-apart, and small surface wobble between poses.
- Squash/stretch preserves apparent volume. A coil extrusion must draw mass from the sphere and visibly merge back, not appear from nowhere.
- Stepped rotation lets thumbprint dimples travel around the surface while the center stays fixed.
- Practical miniature light reveals changing relief and sticky seams.
