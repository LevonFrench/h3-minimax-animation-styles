# Animation style 72

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 clay animation composed for 864x480, using softly sculpted plasticine, visible thumbprints, practical miniature lighting, gentle surface wobble, and stepped movement. A single red clay sphere remains perfectly centered on a neutral clay background at constant size with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere squashes downward, stretches upward, and develops a few thumbprint dimples that crawl around the surface as it slowly turns in place. A tiny clay coil extrudes briefly from the top, curls around the sphere like a ring, then merges back into the body so the sphere ends as a clean centered ball once again.

## Soundscape

Sticky clay presses, gentle peel sounds, and soft squashing impacts create a tactile sculpted feel.
