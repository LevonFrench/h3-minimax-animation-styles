# Spicy — Seamless paint-on-glass loop

## Prompt

**FORMAT:** MiniMax H3, 736×416, 16:9, 24 fps, 124 frames, 5.17 seconds, 100 BPM. Design one uninterrupted 5.17-second cycle and match all visual and musical phase at the boundary.

**STYLE LOCK:** Seamless paint-on-glass loop. backlit wet pigment; counter-rotating spirals; frame-by-frame repaint; brush-pressure ridges; edge-wrapped current; material recurrence; no clean digital dissolve. Build a circular flow field whose pigment density and edge marks recur; disguise the seam with continuous brush motion instead of pausing or crossfading.

**SHOT CLASS:** locked overhead glass-table view; no camera movement. One physically continuous shot; no unmotivated reframing, zoom, orbit, or generic camera drift.

**SUBJECT AND PRIMARY ACTION:** using luminous backlighting, wet oil pigment, visible brush pressure, smeared edges, frame-by-frame repainting, and continuous fluid metamorphosis. Deep blue, turquoise, white, and amber currents fill the entire frame. Two painted spirals rotate in opposite directions while smaller pigment streams flow between them. Amber droplets stretch into fish-like silhouettes, swim in a circular path around the center, and dissolve back into identical droplets. Brush marks entering the right edge continue naturally from the left edge, creating a spatially tileable texture. At 5.17 seconds, after exactly one complete current cycle, every spiral, droplet, streak, pigment density, and brush edge returns to the precise opening state while maintaining uninterrupted motion across the loop boundary.

**TIME BLOCK:** Beats 1–2 / 0.00–1.20 s: establish the repeating phase already in motion. Beats 3–5 / 1.20–3.00 s: carry the single cycle through its widest deformation. Beats 6–7 / 3.00–4.20 s: return all elements toward their opening positions. Beat 8 and tail / 4.20–5.17 s: close the cycle by restoring every position, rotation, texture, light, and motion phase exactly at 5.17 seconds; continuous motion through the boundary, no pause, fade, or final hold.

**SPATIAL BLOCK:** Primary subject begins at stage origin (0,0,0) and remains inside the central title-safe 70% of frame; camera follows the declared shot class without crossing the subject axis. Graphic or rostrum work uses the paper/screen plane as Z=0. Miniature or dimensional work preserves grounded contact and consistent parallax.

**LIGHT AND CONTINUITY:** One coherent lighting progression, stable palette, stable subject design, and persistent material properties. Preserve silhouettes, joint logic, contact shadows, line or grain behavior, and exact title spelling. Transformations must pass through visible intermediate shapes; no cross-dissolves, spontaneous duplicate subjects, broken anatomy, texture swimming, random flicker, or late camera movement.

## Soundscape

Soft water movement, wet brushes dragging across glass, low bubbles, and delicate fluid sweeps form a cyclical ambience with no distinct beginning or ending. Bind each transient to its matching visible contact or material event; no invented impacts.

## Music

Slow glass harmonics, bowed tones, and soft hand percussion form an exact 5.17-second circular phrase whose final sustained texture reconnects seamlessly to its opening. Keep the cue on a 100 BPM grid and make its last sample reconnect to its first at 5.17 seconds without a cadence, pause, or reverb tail.
