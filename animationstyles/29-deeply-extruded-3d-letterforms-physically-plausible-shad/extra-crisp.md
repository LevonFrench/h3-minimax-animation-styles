# Title

ORBIT Letter Portal

## Intent

Travel through a genuinely extruded letter portal and land on one grounded, readable title with invariant light direction.

## Shot Class

Centered 35 mm dolly, shallow depth of field, one continuous forward path.

## Subject / Continuity

Repeated identical extruded uppercase word “ORBIT” forms a tunnel. Every letter has the same beveled face, depth, baseline, and legible topology; the O is a true open portal.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): nearest word fills frame. Beats 2-5 (0.60-3.00): camera dollies forward through the open O, crossing repeated word layers with depth parallax and focal-plane travel. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): camera exits toward one final grounded ORBIT title. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): title makes one 2 cm baseline settle and stops. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Word layers centered on Z=0,+0.6,+1.2,+1.8 m; camera (0,0,−1.0) to (0,0,+1.35), 35 mm, exact central axis.

## Look + Lighting

Deep extrusion, crisp bevels, geometric cast shadows, physically plausible cobalt key and warm rim, restrained focus travel. Shadow direction stays down-right.

## Sound

Silent; no voice, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One dolly. Exact word only: ORBIT. No changing letterforms, blocked O aperture, extra text, camera roll, cuts, changing shadow direction, logos, or motion after 4.80 s.
