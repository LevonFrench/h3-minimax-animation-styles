# Spicy — Editorial photocopy collage

## Prompt

**FORMAT:** MiniMax H3, 736×416, 16:9, 24 fps, 124 frames, 5.17 seconds, 100 BPM. Beat 8 lands at 4.80 seconds; hold the resolved endpoint through the remaining tail.

**STYLE LOCK:** Editorial photocopy collage. torn photo fiber; toner grain; coarse halftone; marker stroke; tape edge; rubber-stamp registration; high-contrast paper palette; page-flip transition. Treat masks as physical paper edges with small cast shadows; preserve cyclist anatomy across strips and make the final frame a deliberately balanced poster.

**SHOT CLASS:** top-down 60 mm rostrum view; one physical page flip, then locked. One physically continuous shot; no unmotivated reframing, zoom, orbit, or generic camera drift.

**SUBJECT AND PRIMARY ACTION:** combining scanned paper, torn photographic fragments, marker strokes, halftone texture, masking, photocopier grain, and fast After Effects-style collage transitions. A generic black-and-white photograph of a city cyclist tears into three vertical strips. The strips slide apart to reveal a bright yellow paper field beneath them. Hand-drawn arrows race around the cyclist, a red circle stamps over one wheel, and oversized cut-paper letters assemble into the exact title "KEEP THE CITY MOVING". The frame flips like a physical page, briefly exposing tape, registration marks, and rough edges, then lands on a balanced poster composition.

**TIME BLOCK:** Beats 1–2 / 0.00–1.20 s: establish the subject, medium, and anticipation with a readable silhouette. Beats 3–5 / 1.20–3.00 s: perform the single main action in physically connected intermediate states. Beats 6–7 / 3.00–4.20 s: resolve the action and allow secondary material motion to settle. Beat 8 / 4.20–4.80 s: land the final composition. Tail / 4.80–5.17 s: stable endpoint; only medium-native micro-motion may continue.

**SPATIAL BLOCK:** Primary subject begins at stage origin (0,0,0) and remains inside the central title-safe 70% of frame; camera follows the declared shot class without crossing the subject axis. Graphic or rostrum work uses the paper/screen plane as Z=0. Miniature or dimensional work preserves grounded contact and consistent parallax.

**LIGHT AND CONTINUITY:** One coherent lighting progression, stable palette, stable subject design, and persistent material properties. Preserve silhouettes, joint logic, contact shadows, line or grain behavior, and exact title spelling. Transformations must pass through visible intermediate shapes; no cross-dissolves, spontaneous duplicate subjects, broken anatomy, texture swimming, random flicker, or late camera movement.

## Soundscape

Paper tears, tape peels, marker scratches, a rubber-stamp impact, and rapid page flips create a tactile editorial sound bed. Bind each transient to its matching visible contact or material event; no invented impacts.

## Music

Chopped drums, upright-bass samples, handclaps, and short brass fragments create a brisk editorial montage rhythm. Keep the cue on a 100 BPM grid, let major accents support the declared beat phases, and resolve by 4.80 seconds so the final visual hold remains clean.
