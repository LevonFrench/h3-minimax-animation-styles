# Animation style 60

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 animation composed for 864x480, using ornate silhouette cutout technique with flat black paper shapes, subtle paper texture, and a softly glowing amber background. A single black circular sphere remains perfectly centered in frame at a constant scale with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere opens into four curved paper flaps like a flower, each flap hinging outward in stepped articulated motion, then the flaps close neatly and reform the original perfect circle. Tiny decorative cut-paper stars drift outward from the opening and settle back into the sphere before the final frame.

## Soundscape

Delicate paper clicks, soft hinge-like taps, and a faint theatrical room tone support the articulated movement.
