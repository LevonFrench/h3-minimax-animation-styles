# Research — Silhouette Cutout Animation

## Actionable vocabulary

- Silhouette animation uses flat opaque profiles, articulated joints or hinges, layered cut-paper shapes, and strong negative space against an illuminated field.
- Lotte Reiniger is a key pioneer; her work favors elegant contour, decorative cut detail, and readable planar articulation.
- For the sphere-flower action, show visible hinge logic and stepped flap motion. Tiny stars should remain paper cutouts with consistent scale and plane.
- Use amber transmitted light, paper fibers, and shallow layer shadows; avoid volumetric CGI or flexible rubber motion.
