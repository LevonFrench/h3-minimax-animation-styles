# Research — Flour-Sack Weight and Force

## Actionable vocabulary

- The flour sack is a classic body-mechanics exercise: emotion and effort must read through silhouette, line of action, compression, and timing without facial detail.
- Anticipation precedes the lift; the planted lower mass compresses while the torso stretches around the heavy cube. The cube should move little to communicate weight.
- Follow-through occurs after failure: the sack collapses, then internal fabric mass rebounds in decreasing ripples.
- Maintain volume and keep the cube rigid. Contrasting deformable cloth with an undeformed iron block creates the weight relationship.
