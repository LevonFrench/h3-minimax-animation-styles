# Title

Flour Sack Versus Iron Bell

## Intent

Express effort and failure without a face by contrasting deformable cloth mass with a rigid heavy object.

## Shot Class

Full-body side view, 50 mm equivalent, locked camera.

## Subject / Continuity

One cream flour-sack character attempts to lift one rigid black iron bell. Sack volume, seam placement, bell geometry, floor contact, and silhouettes remain stable.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): sack leans toward bell. Beat 2 (0.60-1.20): planted lower mass compresses in anticipation. Beats 3-5 (1.20-3.00): torso stretches around handle and strains upward while bell rises no more than 0.02 m. Beat 6 (3.00-3.60): grip fails; bell returns rigidly. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): sack collapses around its planted base. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): one decreasing internal fabric ripple settles. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Sack centered (−0.14,0,0), bell (+0.15,0,0); sack top Y=0.22-0.42 m, bell vertical travel ≤0.02 m. Camera fixed at (0,+0.22,−1.7).

## Look + Lighting

Confident ink contour, opaque cel color, clear line of action, volume-preserving cloth deformation, warm theatrical key, stable floor shadow.

## Sound

Silent; no exertion, impact, music, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One failed lift. No face, cube, successful hoist, bending bell, sliding feet, cuts, camera motion, text, logos, or residual ripple after hold.
