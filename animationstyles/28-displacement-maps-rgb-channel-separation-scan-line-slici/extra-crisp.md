# Title

SIGNAL LOST / RESTORED

## Intent

Corrupt only one title layer in bounded bands, then use a single visible sync bar to restore exact geometry and channel registration.

## Shot Class

Full-frame title plate, locked orthographic camera.

## Subject / Continuity

Exact uppercase title “SIGNAL RESTORED” sits white on black. Background remains perfectly stable while only glyph layers glitch.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): title is pristine. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): three narrow scan-line slices offset horizontally with restrained RGB separation. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): bounded block compression and temporal displacement increase inside those bands only. Beat 6 (3.00-3.60): corruption reaches maximum but title remains recognizable. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): one white vertical sync bar sweeps left-to-right, restoring geometry behind it. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): channels register perfectly. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Title centered at (0,0,0), 0.72 m wide; sync bar X=−0.48 to +0.48 m. Camera fixed at (0,0,−1.4).

## Look + Lighting

Scan-line slicing, RGB offset, block compression, temporal data smear, crisp sync bar, stable post-glitch lockup. Flat electronic black-white field.

## Sound

Silent; no static, dialogue, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

Exact text only: SIGNAL RESTORED. No whole-frame corruption, camera motion, cuts, extra words, misspelling, logos, or residual glitch after 4.20 s.
