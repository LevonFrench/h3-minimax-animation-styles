# Title

Parcel at the Blue Door

## Intent

Stage one reluctant decision through reach, withdrawal, backward anticipation, and committed passage, with delayed parcel motion.

## Shot Class

Medium full miniature profile, 60 mm equivalent, locked camera.

## Subject / Continuity

A clay courier in a fixed mustard coat holds one square parcel before a dark blue hinged door. Preserve feet, face identity, parcel geometry, doorway, hinge, and practical shadows.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): hand reaches toward door. Beat 2 (0.60-1.20): courier withdraws and listens with both feet planted. Beat 3 (1.20-1.80): parcel lifts as a shield, lagging torso by one stepped pose. Beat 4 (1.80-2.40): body leans backward in anticipation. Beats 5-7 (2.40-4.20): courier commits through doorway in one forward action; door opens on stable hinge and parcel trails slightly. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): courier stops just inside. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Courier (−0.28,0,0) to (+0.18,0,+0.18); parcel offset +0.10 m from torso; door fixed at X=0. Camera fixed at (0,+0.28,−1.65).

## Look + Lighting

Tactile plasticine, visible fingerprints, sparse replacement face at listening extreme only, practical warm exterior and cool interior light, persistent contact shadows.

## Sound

Silent visual take; no interior sound, dialogue, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One decision-and-entry action. No repeated hesitation, sliding feet, deforming parcel, unstable doorway, cuts, camera motion, text, logos, or endpoint movement.
