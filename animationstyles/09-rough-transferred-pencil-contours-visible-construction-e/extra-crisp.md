# Title

The Teacup Skater

## Intent

Preserve xerographic pencil energy while one controlled glide culminates in a single compressed landing.

## Shot Class

Full-figure side view, 48 mm equivalent, continuous lateral track at character speed.

## Subject / Continuity

A small teal teacup on two red roller skates, rough transferred graphite contour over stable flat cel paint. Handle, rim ellipse, skate count, and colors remain fixed.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): teacup leans forward in anticipation. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): it glides right, wheels showing subtle ellipse wobble. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): one shallow paper ridge launches it into a stretched airborne smear. Beat 6 (3.00-3.60): body reaches the apex. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): it descends with short speed lines. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): both skates land once; cup compresses then resolves. Hold final pose through 5.1667 s. Camera tracks continuously right, stopping with the landing.

## Spatial Block

Cup (−0.55,0,0) to (+0.42,0,0), apex Y=+0.20 m. Camera (−0.42,+0.18,−1.7) to (+0.55,+0.18,−1.7), parallel to action.

## Look + Lighting

Dry irregular xerographic line, visible construction energy, flat teal/red cel paint, one broad smear at launch, restrained speed lines, slight color-registration imperfection. Neutral paper-stage lighting remains stable.

## Sound

Silent visual take; no dialogue, music, wheel noise, impact, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One glide-jump-land action, one track. No vehicle, pothole, realistic ceramics, extra limbs, changing color, repeated impacts, cuts, zoom, text, logos, or motion after landing.
