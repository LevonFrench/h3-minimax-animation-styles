# Animation style 55

## Prompt

A5.00-second 16:9 stylized feature-quality CG animation composed for 864x480, maintaining the same clean topology, smooth spline animation, soft physically based lighting, and clearly separated secondary motion. An original long-eared desert creature hears a sound behind it. Its eyes shift first, followed by the head, neck, shoulders, torso, and hips in overlapping succession. The long ears remain behind for several frames before swinging through their own ares. A heavy tail begins. turning last, crosses the body's final direction and rebounds. The creature stops facing backward while the ears continue wobbling and the tail completes two decreasing swings. Each connected part follows the leading mass rather than moving simultaneously.

## Soundscape

Dry wind, a distant pebble tap, soft paw movement, ear flutters, and the tail brushing sand make the overlapping motion audible.

## Music

Sparse wooden percussion and low plucked strings follow the sequential movement from eyes through tail.
