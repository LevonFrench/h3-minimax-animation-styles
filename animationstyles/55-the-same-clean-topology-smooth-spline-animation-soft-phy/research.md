# Research — Overlapping Rotation in CG

## Actionable vocabulary

- Lead the turn with attention: eyes, head, neck, shoulders, torso, hips. Connected appendages lag according to mass and flexibility.
- Long ears and a heavy tail need distinct arcs, overshoot, reversal, and damping; they should not begin or stop together.
- Maintain foot contact and body volume while hips counterbalance the upper turn.
- A locked camera and simple environment make successive delays readable.
