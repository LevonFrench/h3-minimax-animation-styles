# Research — Per-character kinetic typography

## Identification and provenance

Kinetic typography is a broad motion-design practice; sources support title design and text-animation controls, not the exact phrase.

## Historically and technically grounded mechanics

Range selectors, baseline and cap-height discipline, per-character position, shallow 3D rotation, tracking changes, controlled overshoot, and motion blur animate type while preserving legibility.

## Actionable motion, material, and camera vocabulary

- Preserve material evidence, edge behavior, motivated overlap, readable spacing, and a stable endpoint.
- Move letters as characters rather than drifting whole words; finish every overshoot before the exact final lockup and keep guide lines subordinate.
- Camera vocabulary: locked orthographic layout view, zero amplitude, zero speed; continuous screen direction; no unmotivated reframing.

## Prompt implications

Name visible causes, not style alone. Preserve the original subject/action, use legible intermediate states and coherent space, and reserve the final tail for a settled hold.
