# Research — Broadcast sports-package motion

## Identification and provenance

This is a contemporary broadcast design category whose visual language varies by network; sources support motion-graphics and branded broadcast workflows.

## Historically and technically grounded mechanics

Angular masks, red-charcoal contrast, radial track diagrams, light streaks, motion-blurred type, flash-motivated stingers, and shallow 3D panel tunnels create compressed broadcast urgency.

## Actionable motion, material, and camera vocabulary

- Preserve material evidence, edge behavior, motivated overlap, readable spacing, and a stable endpoint.
- Keep one hero runner and one continuous package trajectory; make the flash a brief exposure accent, maintain legible shoe/data inserts, and lock the exact title/subtitle.
- Camera vocabulary: fast forward tunnel move, 18% depth amplitude, accelerating then braking; continuous screen direction; no unmotivated reframing.

## Prompt implications

Name visible causes, not style alone. Preserve the original subject/action, use legible intermediate states and coherent space, and reserve the final tail for a settled hold.
