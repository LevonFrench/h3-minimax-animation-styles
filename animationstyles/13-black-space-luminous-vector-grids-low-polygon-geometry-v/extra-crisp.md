# Title

Polygon Manta Through the Lattice

## Intent

Contrast rigid faceted articulation with a mathematically smooth camera path in an early-CG void.

## Shot Class

Wide chase view, 28 mm equivalent, continuous spline dolly with exact zero roll.

## Subject / Continuity

One cyan low-polygon manta glides through a magenta wireframe lattice. Preserve mesh topology, fin joints, facet count, grid vanishing point, and primitive particle count.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): manta holds centered, fins level. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): rigid fin joints rotate downward in two visible increments. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): manta accelerates forward; joint interpolation smooths while facets remain visible. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): it passes through one octagonal grid aperture, leaving a short square-particle trail. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): fins return level at the next lattice plane. Hold through 5.1667 s. Camera follows one smooth forward spline and lands without roll.

## Spatial Block

Manta (0,0,0) to (0,+0.05,+3.0); camera (0,+0.15,−1.2) to (0,+0.20,+1.8), 28 mm, fixed vanishing point.

## Look + Lighting

Black space, luminous vector grid, faceted mesh, Gouraud shading, hierarchical joints, primitive square particles. Cyan key brightens slightly during acceleration.

## Sound

Silent take; no dialogue, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One forward glide. No bird, feathers, organic skin, modern ray tracing, grid warping, camera roll, cuts, text, logos, extra creatures, or motion after 4.80 s.
