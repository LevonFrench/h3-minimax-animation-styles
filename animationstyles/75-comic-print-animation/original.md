# Animation style 75

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 comic-print animation composed for 864x480, using inked contours, halftone dots, offset color registration, graphic impact frames, and punchy panel-like design. A single yellow sphere remains perfectly centered at constant size on a pop-colored background with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere spins rapidly, causing cyan and magenta registration shadows to separate from the main yellow body. It hits a freeze-frame impact pose surrounded by stylized radial lines and a one-frame starburst, then resumes motion and settles back into a clean centered sphere with the registration aligned again.

## Soundscape

Fast swishes, print-like slap sounds, and one crisp impact accent support the graphic freeze frame.

## Music

Punchy breakbeat drums, brass hits, and elastic bass give the sphere a high-energy graphic cadence.
