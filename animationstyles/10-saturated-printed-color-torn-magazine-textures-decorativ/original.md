# Animation style 10

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 paper-collage animation composed for 864x480, using saturated printed color, torn magazine textures, decorative patterns, shallow rostrum-camera parallax, playful scale changes, and surreal replacement transitions. A patterned paper bird flies across a flat orange sky. Each wingbeat replaces the wings with different printed materials: newspaper, floral cloth, star charts, and metallic foil. The bird dives into a circular sun, which opens like an eye. It emerges as a tiny paper submarine swimming through a blue field of floating typography and finishes inside a postage-stamp frame.

## Soundscape

Layered paper flutters, scissors snip, adhesive peels, bubbles rise, and a small stamp lands with a dry tap.

## Music

Psychedelic electric organ, reversed guitar, hand percussion, and tape-looped flute drift through playful stereo movement. @StVie @SuBECT @ ACTION
