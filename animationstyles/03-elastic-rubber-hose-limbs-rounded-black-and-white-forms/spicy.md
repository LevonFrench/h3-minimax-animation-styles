Build motion around a strong downbeat pose, elastic C- and S-curves, and a clean recoil; synchronized impacts should be few and unmistakable.

**SHOT CLASS:** locked waist-high three-quarter view, 40 mm equivalent; gentle gate weave only. One physically continuous shot; no unmotivated reframing, zoom, orbit, or generic camera drift.

**SUBJECT AND PRIMARY ACTION:** using elastic rubber-hose limbs, rounded black-and-white forms, looping rhythmic bounce, high-contrast ink shapes, gentle gate weave, and synchronized musical action. An original bean-shaped street sweeper marches beside a trash bin whose lid behaves like a cymbal. The sweeper's knees pump in repeating arcs, both arms stretch around the bin, and the broom bends deeply before snapping straight. On the final beat, the bin compresses through exaggerated squash, launches the rubbish upward, and catches every piece neatly beneath its lid.
