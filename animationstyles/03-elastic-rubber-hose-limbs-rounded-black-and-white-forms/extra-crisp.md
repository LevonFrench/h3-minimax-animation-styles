# Title

Moon Bell Recoil

## Intent

Build one unmistakable musical-cartoon impact from elastic C-curves, a single downbeat strike, and a clean recoil.

## Shot Class

Full-body medium wide, 40 mm equivalent, locked tripod, eye-level theatrical view.

## Subject / Continuity

A monochrome crescent-moon bellringer with pie-cut eyes, white gloves, rounded shoes, and continuous noodle arms stands beside one oversized bell rope.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): figure settles into a bowed anticipation pose. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): knees pump and both arms stretch upward along one S-curve. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): the figure pulls the rope down as its body elongates, then compresses. Beat 6 (3.00-3.60): bell swings and strikes once. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): limbs recoil in a broad reverse C-curve. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): figure lands in a proud held pose. Hold through 5.1667 s; camera locked.

## Spatial Block

Figure starts and ends at (0,0,0), vertical deformation confined to Y=0-0.42 m; rope at X=+0.16. Camera fixed at (0,+0.22,-1.6), 40 mm, no pan or push.

## Look + Lighting

Black-and-white ink-and-paint, pie-cut eyes, white gloves, elastic limbs without elbows, subtle gate weave and cel flicker. Flat theatrical key with one pale rim brightening at the bell strike.

## Sound

Silent take. No music, dialogue, foley, or generated bell sound.

## Limits

One rope-pull action and one bell impact. No repeated bouncing, extra characters, modern color, skeletal elbows, 3D shading, cuts, camera drift, captions, logos, or moving endpoint.
