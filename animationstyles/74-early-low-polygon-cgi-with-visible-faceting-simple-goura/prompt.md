# Animation style 74

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 computer-animation test composed for 864x480, using early low-polygon CGI with visible faceting, simple Gouraud shading, black background, and mathematically smooth motion. A single faceted metallic sphere remains perfectly centered at a constant size with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere rotates in place, revealing triangular facets and shifting reflected light. Its wireframe briefly overlays the shaded surface, then fades. The sphere opens into latitudinal bands that separate slightly, rotate independently, and rejoin exactly into the original faceted ball by the end.

## Soundscape

Soft servo turns, digital tones, and light crystalline clicks accompany the segmented computer motion.

## Music

Arpeggiated analog synths and restrained electronic percussion evoke an early computer-graphics demo aesthetic.
