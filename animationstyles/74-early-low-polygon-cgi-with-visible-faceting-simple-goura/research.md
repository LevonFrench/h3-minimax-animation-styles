# Research — Early Low-Polygon CGI

## Actionable vocabulary

- Early real-time/CG demo aesthetics use visibly faceted low-poly meshes, simple vertex-interpolated Gouraud shading, black voids, limited lights, and mathematically smooth transforms.
- A wireframe overlay should reveal stable latitude/longitude topology before fading; it should match the shaded mesh exactly.
- Separating latitudinal bands requires rigid, evenly spaced geometry and coordinated independent rotation, followed by precise reassembly.
- Avoid contemporary PBR polish, ray-traced reflections, depth fog, and high-density subdivision.
