# Animation style 67

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 animation composed for 864x480, using luminous sand-on-glass technique with visible grains, subtractive drawing, soft amber backlight, and flowing hand-made transformation. A single dark sand sphere remains perfectly centered on the glowing background at constant size with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere dissolves into a ring of scattered grains, then a spiral, then regathers into the same centered circular mass. Small granular rays pulse outward evenly on all sides, then collapse back inward. By the final frame, the grain density, outline, and center position exactly match the opening sphere.

## Soundscape

Fine granular scraping, soft sweeping motions, and faint dry settling sounds accompany the sand transformations.

## Music

Frame drum, breathy flute, and low sustained tones support the meditative granular motion.
