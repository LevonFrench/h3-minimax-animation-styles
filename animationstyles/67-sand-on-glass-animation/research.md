# Research — Sand-on-glass animation

## Provenance and identification

Sand animation is an established under-camera technique: grains are added, removed, and redistributed between exposures, often over a luminous surface. The NFB documents Caroline Leaf's sand practice and preserves her film *Sand or Peter and the Wolf*; the Academy guide names sand among frame-by-frame media. These sources support the process, not this exact abstract sphere sequence.

## Visual mechanics

- Transform by redistributing a finite field of visible grains: mass → ring → spiral → mass.
- Use subtractive clearing and denser deposits to expose amber backlight.
- Keep granular rays evenly radial and bring their grains back inward.
- Match opening grain density, boundary, center, and apparent mass at the end.

## Prompt implications

Describe grain conservation and hand-swept transitions. Avoid liquid morphing, particle-system depth, or unexplained loss of sand.
