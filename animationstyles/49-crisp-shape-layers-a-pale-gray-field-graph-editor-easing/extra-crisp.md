# Title

Value Graph: Teal Circle

## Intent

Show direct correspondence between one S-shaped position curve and a circle's eased travel with a single corrective settle.

## Shot Class

Full-frame instructional graphic, locked orthographic camera.

## Subject / Continuity

One teal circle moves horizontally on pale gray toward one coral target line; a thin visible value graph maps the same position over time.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): circle holds left; value curve tangent nearly horizontal. Beats 2-5 (0.60-3.00): circle follows one clean Bezier ease while a bright trace advances along the S-shaped graph. Beat 6 (3.00-3.60): position crosses target by 0.02 m and curve crosses target value. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): one corrective settle returns both to exact target. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): diagram holds through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Circle (−0.44,0,0) to (+0.30,0,0), fixed Y; graph occupies lower third, target X=+0.30. Camera fixed at (0,0,−1.5).

## Look + Lighting

Crisp shape layers, teal/coral/pale-gray palette, thin S-curve overlay, exact geometric alignment, flat even illumination.

## Sound

Silent; no voice, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One motion and one overshoot. No repeated spring, vertical drift, speed graph, mismatched overlay, cuts, camera motion, text labels, logos, or endpoint movement.
