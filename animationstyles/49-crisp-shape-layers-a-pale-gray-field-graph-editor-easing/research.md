# Research: vector easing and motion-spacing demonstration

## Identification and provenance

This original is text-identical to folder 48; there is no factual or historical difference to invent. It is a didactic motion-design exercise visualizing slow-in/slow-out, acceleration, overshoot, and settle. Adobe explains that motion-path dots correspond to frames, with tighter spacing at low speed and wider spacing at high speed.

## Visual and motion mechanics

- Preserve a straight horizontal trajectory and constant circular geometry.
- Emphasize the speed profile through positional spacing and a restrained velocity ghost, while keeping blur controlled.
- One overshoot and one correction communicate a tuned graph-editor curve.
- The dotted path should appear as a temporary analytical overlay, then vanish before the hold.
