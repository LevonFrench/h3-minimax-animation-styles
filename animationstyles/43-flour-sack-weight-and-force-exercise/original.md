# Animation style 43

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 traditional hand-drawn cel-animation test composed for 864x480, using the same confident ink contours, opaque painted color, warm studio background, smooth animation on ones, and strict preservation of apparent volume. An original flour-sack character stands beside a heavy iron cube. It crouches deeply, compressing into a wide squat through a clear anticipation pose, then wraps both upper corners around the cube. As it strains upward, its torso stretches vertically while its lower body remains compressed against the floor. The cube rises one inch, then drops. The sack collapses into a broad, flattened shape, rebounds upward through two elastic ripples, and returns to its neutral proportions. Every deformation communicates force and weight without changing the character's total volume.

## Soundscape

Cloth creaks and stretches beneath a low exertion sound. The iron cube lands with a dense impact, followed by two soft fabric rebounds.

## Music

Plucked strings and bassoon follow the effort, pause during the lift, and finish with two playful descending notes.
