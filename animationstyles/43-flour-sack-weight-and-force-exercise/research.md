# Research: flour-sack weight and force exercise

## Identification and provenance

This is a classical hand-drawn character-animation principles exercise: a simple sack communicates anticipation, weight, strain, squash and stretch, follow-through, and recovery without facial detail. Disney Animation explicitly identifies these principles; NFB teaching material stresses that animation’s expressive meaning lies in movement between frames. “Flour-sack exercise” is pedagogical shorthand, not a unique aesthetic school.

## Visual and motion mechanics

- Deep crouch creates anticipation and broad contact with the floor before the lift.
- Upper corners behave as arms, wrapping the cube; torso stretch communicates strain while the planted lower mass remains compressed.
- The iron cube should barely move and fall densely, contrasting the elastic sack.
- Collapse, two diminishing ripples, and restored neutral volume show follow-through and recovery.

## Prompt implications

Stage in a clean side-on warm studio. Keep the sack and cube as the only important forms. Do not let squash/stretch change total volume or make the cube rubbery.
