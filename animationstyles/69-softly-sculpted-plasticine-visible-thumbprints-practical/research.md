# Research — Plasticine clay stop motion

## Provenance and identification

Clay animation is a stop-motion process: a physical plastic material is reshaped incrementally and photographed frame by frame. The Academy's guide explicitly distinguishes clay and other three-dimensional stop-motion materials, while MoMA's stop-motion essay emphasizes the visible handmade character of miniature animation. “Softly sculpted plasticine” is material direction rather than a separate historical school.

## Visual mechanics

- Keep thumbprints, small surface wobble, and practical-light changes coherent with real clay.
- Preserve apparent clay volume through squash and stretch.
- Move dimples across the turning surface as remodeling marks, not projected decals.
- Extrude one coil from the same mass, wrap it once, and merge it without residue.

## Prompt implications

Enforce mass continuity and stepped posing. Avoid liquid simulation, rubber gloss, or extra clay appearing from nowhere.
