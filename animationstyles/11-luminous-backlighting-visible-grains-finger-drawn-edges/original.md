# Animation style 11

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 sand animation composed for 864x480, with luminous backlighting, visible grains, finger-drawn edges, additive and subtractive marks, shifting density, and continuous handmade transformation. A caravan of three tiny figures moves across a dark sand dune. Wind sweeps grains from the dune into tall towers, turning the desert into a dense city around them. The travelers stop as windows appear through precise finger taps. One figure raises a hand; the buildings loosen into thousands of grains, curve overhead like a wave, and settle into the silhouette of a protective tree.

## Soundscape

Fine grains scrape over glass, wind builds gradually, distant footsteps cross dry sand, and the final tree settles with a soft granular rush.

## Music

Frame drum, breathy reed flute, and sustained low vocal tones move without a rigid meter.
