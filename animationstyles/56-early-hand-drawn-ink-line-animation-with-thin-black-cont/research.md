# Research — Early ink-line animation and line boil

## Provenance and identification

“Early hand-drawn ink-line” is a broad evocation rather than a precise period attribution. MoMA's early-animation survey documents animation's formative drawn and graphic experiments, while Disney describes the underlying frame-by-frame drawing process. The source's line boil, doubled contour, frame flicker, hatching, and off-white paper are actionable surface mechanics; they should not be attributed to a single inventor without stronger evidence.

## Visual mechanics

- Redraw the contour each frame with slight coherent displacement to create line boil.
- Keep the sphere's center and overall scale invariant while local contour inflation, doubling, and jitter occur.
- Let hatch marks orbit as individual drawn accents, then absorb back into the boundary.
- Mild gate weave/frame flicker should texture the image without moving the subject off registration.

## Prompt implications

Anchor the geometry numerically and separate “living line” motion from object translation. Return the exact contour and hatch-free opening state before the final hold.
