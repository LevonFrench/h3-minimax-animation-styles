# Title

Apricot Moon Delivery

## Intent

Create a fresh stop-motion micro-drama where tactile load transfer and a clean silhouette change make one delivery believable.

## Shot Class

Low medium-wide miniature shot, 45 mm equivalent, continuous 18 cm slider move.

## Subject / Continuity

A carved-wood rabbit courier in a charcoal linen vest rolls one round apricot moon into a shallow rooftop cradle. Fixed replacement face, visible fabric weave, stable prop scale.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): rabbit braces behind the moon. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): shoulders compress and both paws begin one continuous push. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): moon rolls up the short wooden incline in stepped increments. Beat 6 (3.00-3.60): rear foot plants harder as the moon crests. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): moon drops into the cradle with one contact jolt. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): rabbit releases into a stable upright silhouette. Hold through 5.1667 s. Camera slides continuously right and settles by 4.80.

## Spatial Block

Rabbit (−0.25,0,0) to (+0.05,0,+0.03); moon (−0.10,+0.08,0) to (+0.28,+0.21,+0.06). Camera (−0.35,+0.18,−1.15) to (−0.17,+0.18,−1.15).

## Look + Lighting

Tactile carved wood, linen fibers, thumb-worn paint, practical rooftop miniature, persistent contact shadows, slight registration flutter. Peach practical glow rises gently as the moon seats.

## Sound

Silent visual take; no dialogue, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One push action and one continuous slider path. No railway, suitcase, multiple facial swaps, sliding feet, liquid motion, cuts, zoom, text, logos, extra characters, or endpoint movement.
