# Title

Pencil Mallet Strike

## Intent

Reveal anticipation spacing, shoulder-centered arc, impact, follow-through, and recovery in one rough animation drawing.

## Shot Class

Top-down full-page study, locked animation-stand camera.

## Subject / Continuity

A faceless rough-pencil worker swings one wooden mallet toward one peg. Blue construction, graphite mass, red arc corrections, onion skins, and fixed peg holes persist.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beats 1-2 (0.00-1.20): raised anticipation extreme with close drawing spacing. Beats 3-5 (1.20-3.00): hands and mallet accelerate on one shoulder-centered arc, spacing widening as torso bends and hips counterbalance. Beat 6 (3.00-3.60): one clear contact drawing. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): mallet follows through beyond peg. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): worker recovers to stable low pose. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Worker rooted at (−0.12,0,0); mallet head arcs from (−0.18,+0.38,0) through (+0.15,+0.12,0). Camera fixed at (0,+0.72,0).

## Look + Lighting

Rough graphite, blue underdrawing, purposeful red corrections, onion-skin arc evidence, paper tooth, fixed registration.

## Sound

Silent; no impact, voice, music, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One strike. No random scribble, repeated impacts, mallet deformation, sliding feet, camera motion, cuts, text, logos, or endpoint movement.
