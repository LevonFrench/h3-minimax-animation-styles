# Research — Rough animation pencil test: mallet weight and arcs

## Provenance and identification

The source is a rough pencil-test workflow focused on force, spacing, and a readable impact pose. Disney's process notes strong poses, timing, weight, mass, and rough-to-cleanup progression; the Academy's guide emphasizes advance planning and frame-by-frame changes. Construction colors, onion skins, timing charts, and registration holes are production cues, not a single historically fixed aesthetic.

## Visual mechanics

- Slow, closely spaced raised anticipation should contrast with increasingly broad downswing spacing.
- Hands orbit the shoulders on a broad arc; torso and hips counterbalance rather than hinge as one rigid slab.
- Preserve one unmistakable contact drawing before follow-through carries the mallet beyond the peg.
- Red corrections should clarify the swing arc and body balance, not decorate every line.

## Prompt implications

Make the peg strike the sole event. Maintain tool length and hand placement across ghosts, and reserve recovery for the latter interval so impact does not blur into the settle.
