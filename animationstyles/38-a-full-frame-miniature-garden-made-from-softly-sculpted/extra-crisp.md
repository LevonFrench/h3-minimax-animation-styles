# Title

Clay Crocus Clockwork

## Intent

Build a second distinct mechanical clay cycle from one unambiguous gear, matching material and shadow phase at the endpoint.

## Shot Class

Top-down three-quarter miniature close-up, 55 mm equivalent, locked camera.

## Subject / Continuity

Six violet plasticine crocuses form a ring around one small brass gear. Every flower has fixed petal identity, visible thumbprints, and planted stem contact.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): all crocuses closed at phase zero. Beats 2-7 (0.60-4.20): gear turns exactly one revolution in stepped increments, opening each flower sequentially clockwise and then closing it as the next opens, a single propagated mechanical phase. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): gear tooth, petals, stem bends, and shadows match opening state. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Gear centered at (0,0,0); flowers on radius 0.24 m, Y height 0.12 m. Camera fixed at (0,+0.55,−0.55), angled 45 degrees.

## Look + Lighting

Soft plasticine, fingerprints, stepped flower poses, brass gear, practical miniature light, slight tactile wobble. Fixed warm key preserves seam shadows.

## Sound

Silent; no dialogue, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One propagated bloom cycle. No bee, sunflower, extra gears, identity drift, floating petals, camera movement, text, logos, or motion after the final state. Loop precision verified in post.
