# Spicy — Mechanical clay-animation loop

## Prompt

**FORMAT:** MiniMax H3, 736×416, 16:9, 24 fps, 124 frames, 5.17 seconds, 100 BPM. Design one uninterrupted 5.17-second cycle and match all visual and musical phase at the boundary.

**STYLE LOCK:** Mechanical clay-animation loop. soft plasticine; visible thumbprints; stepped flower poses; brass gear phase; miniature practical light; tactile wobble; bee flight cycle; seamless endpoint. Keep the gear as one unambiguous mechanism and retain material imperfections without identity drift; match petal, bee, tooth, and shadow phase at the boundary.

**SHOT CLASS:** locked miniature three-quarter macro view, 65 mm equivalent. One physically continuous shot; no unmotivated reframing, zoom, orbit, or generic camera drift.

**SUBJECT AND PRIMARY ACTION:** showing a full-frame miniature garden made from softly sculpted plasticine with visible thumbprints, practical lighting, tactile surface wobble, stepped movement, and subtle registration variation. Six clay flowers surround a central brass-colored gear. The gear completes exactly one clockwise revolution, driving each flower through a repeating sequence: stems bend outward, petals open, centers rise, petals close, and stems return upright. A small clay bee follows a complete circular flight path through stepped poses, landing on the same flower for one frame before immediately beginning the cycle again. Every gear tooth, petal, stem, bee position, contact shadow, and practical-light flicker matches the opening frame exactly at 5.17 seconds, with no final hold.

**TIME BLOCK:** Beats 1–2 / 0.00–1.20 s: establish the repeating phase already in motion. Beats 3–5 / 1.20–3.00 s: carry the single cycle through its widest deformation. Beats 6–7 / 3.00–4.20 s: return all elements toward their opening positions. Beat 8 and tail / 4.20–5.17 s: close the cycle by restoring every position, rotation, texture, light, and motion phase exactly at 5.17 seconds; continuous motion through the boundary, no pause, fade, or final hold.

**SPATIAL BLOCK:** Primary subject begins at stage origin (0,0,0) and remains inside the central title-safe 70% of frame; camera follows the declared shot class without crossing the subject axis. Graphic or rostrum work uses the paper/screen plane as Z=0. Miniature or dimensional work preserves grounded contact and consistent parallax.

**LIGHT AND CONTINUITY:** One coherent lighting progression, stable palette, stable subject design, and persistent material properties. Preserve silhouettes, joint logic, contact shadows, line or grain behavior, and exact title spelling. Transformations must pass through visible intermediate shapes; no cross-dissolves, spontaneous duplicate subjects, broken anatomy, texture swimming, random flicker, or late camera movement.

## Soundscape

Tiny gear clicks, clay petals peeling open, miniature spring sounds, and a soft buzzing flight circle repeat as one continuous mechanical-organic cycle. Bind each transient to its matching visible contact or material event; no invented impacts.

## Music

A precise 100 BPM loop of toy piano, marimba, pizzicato strings, and muted woodblocks follows the flower sequence and reconnects cleanly at the loop boundary. Keep the cue on a 100 BPM grid and make its last sample reconnect to its first at 5.17 seconds without a cadence, pause, or reverb tail.
