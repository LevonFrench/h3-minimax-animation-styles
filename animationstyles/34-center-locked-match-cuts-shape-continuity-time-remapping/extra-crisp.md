# Title

RING / EYE / ECLIPSE

## Intent

Use center, scale, edge, and clockwise rotational continuity to hide two peak-motion match cuts and reveal one final title.

## Shot Class

Centered macro montage, 70 mm equivalent, locked optical axis.

## Subject / Continuity

A ceramic potter's ring, an owl eye, and a solar eclipse share the exact same central circle, diameter, clockwise rotation direction, and luminance edge.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beats 1-2 (0.00-1.20): potter's ring spins clockwise, accelerating. Beat 3 (1.20-1.80): peak-motion match cut to owl iris at identical center and edge. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): iris continues clockwise with a speed ramp. Beat 6 (3.00-3.60): luminance bridge match-cuts to eclipse corona. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): corona rotation slows, negative space opening exact word “ALIGN.” Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): title locks. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Shared circle fixed at (0,0,0), diameter 0.32 m in frame. Camera fixed at (0,0,−1.2), no change across cuts.

## Look + Lighting

Photographic material changes with exact shape continuity, controlled time remapping, clockwise motion, luminance bridge, crisp negative-space title.

## Sound

Silent; no dialogue, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One continuous rotational action across two cuts. Exact title only: ALIGN. No center shift, scale jump, reverse rotation, extra objects, dissolves, camera movement, logos, or motion after 4.80 s.
