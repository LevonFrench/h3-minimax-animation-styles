# Research — Xerographic cel linework

## Provenance and identification

Xerography changed cel production by transferring drawing lines mechanically rather than requiring every contour to be hand inked. MoMA's production material on animation and the Academy's cel overview ground the cel context; Disney's process explains the rough-drawing, cleanup, and in-between stages whose marks the transferred line can retain. “Dry-brush energy” in the source is an aesthetic analogy, not a literal xerographic mechanism.

## Visual mechanics

- Preserve rough transferred-pencil contour variation over flat orange paint.
- Use one-frame horizontal smear as an authored drawing, followed by immediate shape recovery.
- Modulate line density as successive-cel texture, not global exposure flicker.
- Two decreasing wobbles should resolve at the same center and size.

## Prompt implications

Keep the smear to one frame and count two oscillations. The process cue should live in the contour; do not simulate it merely with noisy video grain.
