# Title

Anamorphic Paper Whale

## Intent

Exploit one measured corner seam and hero viewing zone so a paper whale appears to break across the screen lip.

## Shot Class

Fixed street-level hero viewpoint, 50 mm equivalent, locked physical camera facing a 90-degree corner LED.

## Subject / Continuity

One folded white-paper whale swims inside a virtual cobalt shadow box spanning both LED faces. Physical bezel, corner seam, screen pixels, and street geometry remain visible and registered.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): whale rests deep in box. Beats 2-4 (0.60-2.40): it advances along one diagonal path toward the corner, forced perspective calibrated to the fixed view. Beats 5-6 (2.40-3.60): snout crosses the virtual screen lip with correct occlusion. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): tail clears the seam while the virtual shadow stays anchored. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): whale holds half-emerged. Hold through 5.1667 s; physical camera locked.

## Spatial Block

Whale virtual position (−0.30,+0.15,+0.80) to (+0.08,+0.18,−0.08) relative to screen corner origin; camera fixed at (−4.2,+1.65,−7.0), aimed at seam.

## Look + Lighting

Anamorphic forced perspective, virtual shadow box, edge breakout, LED pixel texture, visible 90-degree seam. Cool virtual fill and one soft white highlight follow the whale.

## Sound

Silent; no crowd, dialogue, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One emergence action. No camera movement, hidden seam, warped bezel, real object leaving screen, extra animals, cuts, text, logos, or endpoint motion.
