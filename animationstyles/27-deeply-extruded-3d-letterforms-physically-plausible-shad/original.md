# Animation style 27

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 typography animation composed for 864x480, using deeply extruded 3D letterforms, physically plausible shadows, smooth camera movement, shallow depth, and clean motion-graphics lighting. The camera travels rapidly through a tunnel constructed from repeated versions of the word "FORWARD". Each layer is offset in depth and slightly rotated, creating rhythmic parallax. The tunnel narrows as the letters align, then the camera passes through the final "O" and emerges into a bright open field. One solid extruded title reading "FORWARD" rotates upright, lands on an invisible baseline with a slight overshoot, and casts a long geometric shadow toward the lower-right corner.

## Soundscape

Layered air passes, deep spatial whooshes, restrained mechanical rotations, and one solid landing impact accompany the camera and title.

## Music

Driving electronic drums and an ascending synthesizer arpeggio increase through the tunnel, resolving into a broad sustained chord at the landing.
