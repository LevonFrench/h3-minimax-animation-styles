# Animation style 26

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 particle-based motion-design animation composed for 864x480, using thousands of fine luminous points, turbulence fields, attractor-driven motion, depth-of-field falloff, and a controlled 3D camera push. A loose cloud of cyan particles drifts through dark space. A moving force passes through the cloud, creating a visible wave that aligns the points into parallel signal bands. The bands twist around one another, flatten toward camera, and gather into the exact word "SIGNAL". A few particles overshoot, curve backward, and settle into the letter edges. A thin pulse travels through the completed title from left to right while the remaining background particles slow almost to stillness.

## Soundscape

Fine granular movement, airy digital sweeps, subtle electrical crackles, and one focused tonal pulse align with the particle gathering.

## Music

A restrained 92 BPM electronic pattern uses deep kicks, granular percussion, and sustained high-frequency pads, resolving when the title forms.
