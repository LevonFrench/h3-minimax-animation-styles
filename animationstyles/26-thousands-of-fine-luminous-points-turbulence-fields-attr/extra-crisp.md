# Title

EMBER Particle Assembly

## Intent

Preserve individual trajectories while one coherent force wave and one attractor gather a stable exact word.

## Shot Class

Deep full-frame particle field, 55 mm equivalent, locked camera.

## Subject / Continuity

Thousands of fine amber emissive points drift in black space. A single central attractor forms exact uppercase word “EMBER”; point count and depth falloff remain coherent.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): stochastic drift establishes. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): one horizontal signal wave aligns points into five depth bands. Beats 4-6 (1.80-3.60): central attraction radius expands once, bending preserved trajectories toward glyph positions. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): points overshoot slightly and return. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): exact word locks, one faint pulse travels left-to-right. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Particles occupy X=±0.60, Y=±0.34, Z=0-1.2 m; attractor at (0,0,+0.30). Camera fixed at (0,0,−1.8), 55 mm.

## Look + Lighting

Fine emissive points, turbulence velocity field, coherent wave, depth falloff, restrained amber bloom on black.

## Sound

Silent; no voice, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One gathering action. Exact word only: EMBER. No particle explosions, random cuts, camera motion, extra words, topology smears, logos, or drift after 4.80 s.
