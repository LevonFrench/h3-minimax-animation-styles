# Animation style 18

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 motion-design test composed for 864x480, using clean After Effects-style text animators, per-character position and scale controls, graph-editor easing, motion blur, and controlled overshoot. On a matte cream background, the word "MAKE" enters one letter at a time from below, each character overshooting vertically before settling into strict alignment. "IT" snaps into the center through a quick tracking expansion. The word "MOVE" stretches across the frame as individual letters rotate forward in shallow 3D, compress on impact, and rebound. Thin black guide lines briefly appear to demonstrate baseline and cap-height alignment, then retract through trim paths. The final title reads exactly "MAKE IT. MOVE" and holds completely stable for the final half-second.

## Soundscape

Dry clicks mark each letter landing, accompanied by two short air swishes and one soft low-frequency impact when the full phrase locks.

## Music

A118 BPM electronic rhythm of muted kicks, rim clicks, and short marimba-like tones follows the staggered letter animation and stops on the final lock.
