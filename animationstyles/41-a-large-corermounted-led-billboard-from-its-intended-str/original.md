# Animation style 41

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 digital-out-of-home loop composed for 864x480, showing a large corner-mounted LED billboard from its intended street viewing angle at night. The animated display presents a deep virtual room using forced perspective. A generic glossy red ball rolls from the rear of the virtual room toward the corner seam, appears to emerge beyond the physical screen, and floats briefly above the street. It stretches into a ribbon, curves around the outer corner, retracts into the billboard, and reforms as the same ball at the exact rear starting position. The virtual room lighting responds continuously to the ball and ribbon. At 5.00 seconds, the ball position, rotation, shape, contact shadow, billboard content, LED brightness, room perspective, surrounding reflections, and animation velocity exactly match the opening frame, creating an uninterrupted public-display loop.

## Soundscape

Nighttime city ambience surrounds a deep rolling sound, a soft spatial whoosh as the object appears to leave the display, and a smooth reverse sweep as it returns. The sound cycle reconnects without a final impact.

## Music

Low electronic percussion and broad spatial synthesizer tones form an exact five-second loop designed for continuous outdoor playback.
