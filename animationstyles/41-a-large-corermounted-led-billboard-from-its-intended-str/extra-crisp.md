# Title

Red Ribbon Corner Breakout

## Intent

Make one red ball cross a virtual proscenium, elongate once, and reform while the physical street viewpoint remains fixed.

## Shot Class

Fixed street hero view, 45 mm equivalent, locked camera facing corner LED.

## Subject / Continuity

One glossy red ball inhabits a white virtual room across a 90-degree LED corner. Preserve seam, pixel structure, bezel, contact shadow, environmental reflections, and street geometry.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): ball rests deep inside. Beats 2-4 (0.60-2.40): it rolls toward viewer on one path with physically registered shadow. Beats 5-6 (2.40-3.60): ball crosses screen lip and elongates once into a short ribbon across the corner seam. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): ribbon retracts behind lip. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): ball reforms and stops inside the virtual room. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Virtual ball (−0.20,+0.12,+0.90) to (+0.05,+0.12,−0.08) and back to (+0.05,+0.12,+0.10). Camera fixed at (−4.0,+1.65,−7.2).

## Look + Lighting

Naked-eye anamorphic forced perspective, virtual room, occlusion at physical frame, LED pixels, realistic night brightness and reflections, one stable softbox highlight.

## Sound

Silent; no street noise, voice, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One roll-elongate-reform action. No hologram, camera movement, hidden seam, multiple objects, extra morphs, cuts, text, logos, or endpoint movement.
