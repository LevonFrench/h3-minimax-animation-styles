# Research — Sand-on-Glass Animation

## Actionable vocabulary

- Sand animation is an under-camera subtractive/additive technique: grains are pushed, swept, thinned, piled, and redrawn against a light table.
- Grain density controls value; finger or card sweeps leave characteristic ridges and translucent channels.
- Transform continuously from filled circle to ring to spiral and back by redistributing the same material. Avoid particle-system explosions or random respawn.
- The final frame should restore density, edge character, scattered-grain pattern, and center.
