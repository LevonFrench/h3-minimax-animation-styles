# Research — Classical cel animation with multiplane depth

## Identification and provenance

“Classical cel” spans many studio practices; these sources support cels, musical animation, and core motion principles.

## Historically and technically grounded mechanics

Stable inked contours and opaque color fields sit over softly painted backgrounds. Foreground, character, and distant planes move at different rates; anticipation, stretch, impact compression, rebound, follow-through, and clean silhouettes carry the acting.

## Actionable motion, material, and camera vocabulary

- Preserve material evidence and edge behavior; use controlled silhouettes, motivated overlap, readable spacing, and a stable endpoint.
- Keep the tailor and spool readable as the sole chase, maintain color registration, and let scissors, character plane, and shelves provide restrained depth rather than competing motion.
- Camera vocabulary: gentle forward multiplane push, 3% scale amplitude, slow ease-in; continuous screen direction; no unmotivated reframing.

## Prompt implications

Translate the technique into visible causes rather than naming the style alone. Prioritize the original subject and action, use one dominant action hierarchy, make transformations pass through legible intermediate states, and reserve the final tail for a settled hold.
