# Title

Lantern Fox at the Reed Gate

## Intent

Use classical cel clarity and multiplane depth to make a single cautious crossing feel theatrical and legible.

## Shot Class

Medium-wide profile, 50 mm equivalent, slow lateral dolly on a multiplane stand.

## Subject / Continuity

A small rust-red fox in a fixed indigo neckerchief carries one glowing paper lantern. Ink contours and opaque cel color remain stable; reeds, fox, gate, and watercolor hills occupy separate planes.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): fox anticipates at stage-left. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): it steps toward the reed gate, lantern held steady. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): body passes beneath the arch while tail follows through. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): fox settles stage-right and raises the lantern slightly. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): lantern reveals a painted moth on the gate. Hold pose, glow, and camera landing through 5.1667 s. Camera tracks continuously left-to-right, easing to rest at 4.80.

## Spatial Block

Fox (−0.45,0,0) to (+0.35,0,0); lantern Y=+0.28 m. Camera (−0.25,+0.24,−2.2) to (+0.22,+0.24,−2.2), never crossing the character plane.

## Look + Lighting

Confident inked contours, opaque cel fills, softly painted watercolor background, foreground reed parallax, warm theatrical lantern key against cool blue dusk. Stable face and costume colors.

## Sound

Silent visual take; no dialogue, music, foley, or generated soundtrack.

## Limits

One crossing action, one continuous dolly. No extra characters, costume changes, digital gradients, volumetric 3D fur, cuts, zoom, text, logos, flicker, or movement after 4.80 s.
