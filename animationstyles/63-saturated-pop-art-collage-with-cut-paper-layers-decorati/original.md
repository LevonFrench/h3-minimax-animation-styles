# Animation style 63

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 animation composed for 864x480, using saturated pop-art collage with cut-paper layers, decorative patterns, screen-printed texture, and fluid surreal transitions. A single sphere remains perfectly centered at constant size with no camera movement and no cuts. Its surface cycles through bold patterned skins —floral, striped, polka-dot, and starburst—while staying the same underlying sphere. The sphere briefly blooms into layered concentric cutouts, then collapses back inward. Color halos pulse behind it in concentric rhythmic bursts, and the final frame restores the first pattern and palette exactly.

## Soundscape

Paper flutters, soft collage snaps, and swirling whooshes accompany the pattern changes and layered expansions.

## Music

Psychedelic electric organ, hand percussion, and reversed guitar textures create a buoyant looping groove.
