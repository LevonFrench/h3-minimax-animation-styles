# Research — Paint-on-Glass Wipe Transformation

## Actionable vocabulary

- Paint-on-glass supports subtractive motion: scrape or wipe wet paint away to expose the light table, then push pigment back into the form.
- Make this variant distinct by emphasizing finger-wiped channels, palette-knife scallops, and counterclockwise repainting rather than repeating the same brush loop.
- Thin pigment glows; thick pigment becomes opaque and textured. Preserve wet edges and visible tool paths.
- The loop must return pigment density, tool-mark orientation, edge softness, and highlight position.
