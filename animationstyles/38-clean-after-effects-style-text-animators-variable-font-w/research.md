# Research — Variable-Font Kinetic Typography

## Actionable vocabulary

- Variable fonts interpolate continuously along registered axes such as weight and width. Animate those axes with text animators and range selectors rather than crossfading between unrelated fonts.
- Per-character tracking compensation keeps the phrase optically centered as glyph width changes. Smooth or round selector shapes produce a traveling breath wave.
- Typography must remain exact and legible. Avoid asking generative video to invent an intermediate misspelling; use only the two approved phrases.
- Pair expansion with increased width/weight/tracking and contraction with reduced values. A radial field can echo breath while remaining subordinate to the words.
