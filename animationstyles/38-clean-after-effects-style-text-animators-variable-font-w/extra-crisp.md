# Title

BREATHE / RELEASE

## Intent

Create one typographic breath wave through registered variable-font axes and per-character tracking compensation.

## Shot Class

Centered full-frame type plate, locked orthographic camera.

## Subject / Continuity

Exact phrase “BREATHE” in a variable sans-serif, white on deep blue. Preserve spelling, counters, center, baseline, and glyph topology.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): regular phrase holds. Beats 2-4 (0.60-2.40): a round left-to-right selector increases weight, width, and tracking as a traveling expansion wave. Beats 5-7 (2.40-4.20): the same selector contracts axes to the opening values with optical centering compensation. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): exact phrase locks. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Phrase centered at (0,0,0), width varying 0.58-0.72 m around a fixed optical center. Camera fixed at (0,0,−1.4).

## Look + Lighting

Clean text animators, continuous registered-axis interpolation, smooth range selector, subordinate radial blue field, crisp typography.

## Sound

Silent; no breath, voice, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

Exact word only: BREATHE. No second phrase, crossfaded fonts, misspelling, broken counters, camera motion, cuts, logos, or motion after 4.80 s.
