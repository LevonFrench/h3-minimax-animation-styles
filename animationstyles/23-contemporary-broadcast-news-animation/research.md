# Research — Broadcast-news information architecture

## Identification and provenance

Broadcast-news graphics are institution-specific; sources support layer, camera, typography, and safe-layout mechanics rather than one universal house style.

## Historically and technically grounded mechanics

Dot globes, transparent data cards, restrained blue-white color, a red perimeter rule, clean sans-serif hierarchy, calibrated depth, and title-safe lockup organize dense information.

## Actionable motion, material, and camera vocabulary

- Preserve material evidence, edge behavior, motivated overlap, readable spacing, and a stable endpoint.
- Limit simultaneous labels, move cards on one coherent depth axis, pass through the final panel once, and finish the red rule before the stable exact headline.
- Camera vocabulary: precise forward data-space dolly, 12% depth amplitude, constant speed with soft stop; continuous screen direction; no unmotivated reframing.

## Prompt implications

Name visible causes, not style alone. Preserve the original subject/action, use legible intermediate states and coherent space, and reserve the final tail for a settled hold.
