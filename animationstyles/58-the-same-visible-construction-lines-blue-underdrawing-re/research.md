# Research — Rough Pencil Flight Arcs

## Actionable vocabulary

- Separate motion paths by body part: torso on a shallow wave, wing tips on large ellipses, stable head, delayed tail counter-arc, and compact foot-tuck arcs.
- Wing spacing tightens at direction reversals and widens through fastest mid-stroke. Two complete cycles should remain countable.
- Landing requires a clear branch contact and one decreasing body settle, not an abrupt freeze.
- Onion skins and colored corrections should clarify arcs and spacing while the registration remains fixed.
