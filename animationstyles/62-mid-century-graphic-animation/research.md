# Research — Mid-century modernist limited graphic animation

## Provenance and identification

The source combines flat mid-century graphic design with selective limited movement. MoMA identifies UPA's postwar work as modernist, flat, symbolic, limited, and less naturalistic; its Hubley material also notes simplified, impressionistic design influenced by modern painting. These sources support the graphic lineage, but not this exact teal-sphere construction as a historical artifact.

## Visual mechanics

- Favor flat restrained blocks, crisp edges, asymmetrical timing, and economical transformations.
- Convert the sphere into disc and rings without faux depth.
- Move the shadow in discrete left/right steps while the sphere remains registered.
- Rectangles should enter in symmetrical balance and withdraw completely.

## Prompt implications

Treat “limited” as selective change and designed holds, not low-quality stutter. Keep palette and geometry disciplined.
