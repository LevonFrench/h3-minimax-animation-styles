# Research — Rough Pencil Hammer Arc

## Actionable vocabulary

- Anticipation uses close drawing spacing at the raised extreme; acceleration widens spacing through the downswing.
- Hands and mallet travel on a shoulder-centered arc while the torso bends in sympathy and hips counterbalance.
- A readable impact needs one clear contact drawing, then follow-through beyond the peg before recovery.
- Use onion skins and red corrections to reveal intended arcs, not as random decorative scribble.
