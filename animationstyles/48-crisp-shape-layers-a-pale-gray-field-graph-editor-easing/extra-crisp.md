# Title

Speed Graph: Coral Circle

## Intent

Make velocity legible through observed spacing, a matching dotted path, and one small overshoot-settle.

## Shot Class

Full-frame instructional graphic, locked orthographic camera.

## Subject / Continuity

One coral circle travels horizontally on a pale gray field toward one navy target line. A thin speed graph and dotted motion path remain secondary and exactly correspond to motion.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): circle at left, speed zero. Beats 2-5 (0.60-3.00): circle eases in, reaches peak velocity, then eases out; path dots spread wide in middle while speed graph rises and falls. Beat 6 (3.00-3.60): circle crosses target by 0.025 m, graph shows a small reverse lobe. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): circle returns to exact target. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): full diagram holds. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Circle center (−0.46,0,0) to (+0.32,0,0), fixed Y=0; target X=+0.32. Camera fixed at (0,0,−1.5).

## Look + Lighting

Crisp vector shapes, pale gray field, coral hero circle, thin navy graph, accurate dotted spacing, clean graph-editor easing.

## Sound

Silent; no instruction voice, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One-dimensional motion. No vertical drift, repeated spring, mismatched dots, camera movement, cuts, text labels, logos, or motion after 4.20 s.
