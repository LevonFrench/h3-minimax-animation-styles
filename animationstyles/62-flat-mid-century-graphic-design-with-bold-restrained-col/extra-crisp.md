# Title

Mid-Century Signal Disc

## Intent

Build visual rhythm from selective ring motion, held central geometry, rectangular accents, and jazz-like pauses.

## Shot Class

Asymmetric full-frame graphic composition, locked orthographic camera.

## Subject / Continuity

One teal central disc remains fixed on cream. Two charcoal rings, one warm-orange shadow step, and three small rectangles are the only moving elements.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): designed stillness. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): outer ring rotates 90 degrees and pauses; inner ring remains held. Beat 4 (1.80-2.40): three rectangles shift one grid unit in sequence. Beats 5-6 (2.40-3.60): inner ring rotates −180 degrees while orange shadow steps once down-right. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): all accents return to balanced positions. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): complete designed hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Disc centered (+0.10,0,0), diameter 0.28 m; accents remain on Z=0 grid. Camera fixed at (0,0,−1.5).

## Look + Lighting

UPA modernist flat shapes, sparse line, designed asymmetry, limited cream/teal/charcoal/orange palette, economical pauses, no naturalistic motion.

## Sound

Silent; no jazz, voice, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One rhythmic graphic cycle. No character, fluid deformation, gradients, extra colors, camera motion, cuts, text, logos, or endpoint movement.
