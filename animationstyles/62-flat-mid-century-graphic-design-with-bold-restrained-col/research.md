# Research — Mid-Century Limited Graphic Animation

## Actionable vocabulary

- UPA modernism favored flat symbolic shapes, bold but restrained color, sparse line, asymmetry or designed balance, and movement that did not attempt to imitate nature.
- Limited animation can hold the central disc while moving only rings, shadow steps, and a few rectangular accents.
- Build visual rhythm through economical shape changes and jazz-like pauses rather than constant fluid deformation.
- Keep palette small: cream, teal, charcoal, and one warm accent.
