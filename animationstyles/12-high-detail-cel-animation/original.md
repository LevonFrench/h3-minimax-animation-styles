# Animation style 12

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 high-detail cel animation composed for 864x480, featuring precise mechanical drawing, dense industrial backgrounds, atmospheric haze, dramatic practical lights, strong perspective, and weighty human movement. An original maintenance rider speeds along the roof of a moving elevated train at night. She crouches beneath a signal gantry, plants one boot, and leaps across the gap between two carriages. Her coat and tool straps trail with delayed overlap. The train suspension reacts beneath her landing, sparks burst from the rails, and the camera tracks beside her while layered city structures rush past at different depths.

## Soundscape

Rail clatter, electric motors, rushing wind, fabric snaps, metal footfalls, and a sharp electrical spark fill the elevated route.

## Music

Driving analog synthesizer bass, gated percussion, and tense low strings build through the jump and release at the landing.
