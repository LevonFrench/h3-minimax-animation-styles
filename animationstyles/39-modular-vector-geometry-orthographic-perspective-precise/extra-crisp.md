# Title

Isometric Parcel Transfer

## Intent

Follow one package through four modular waypoints while maintaining orthographic axes, precise paths, and coherent subsystem phases.

## Shot Class

Wide isometric overview, orthographic camera locked at 30-degree elevation.

## Subject / Continuity

One coral package moves through four fixed navy stations on a cream vector logistics floor. Parallel axes, package scale, belt marks, arm pivots, and shadow direction remain invariant.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): package enters station one. Beats 2-5 (0.60-3.00): steady conveyor moves it through stations two and three; each transfer arm makes one precise quarter-turn as package arrives. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): final arm indexes package into station four with one eased transfer. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): package and all belts stop on registration marks. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Package (−0.55,0,0) to (+0.55,+0.28,+0.20) along fixed isometric path; camera fixed at (+1.8,+1.6,−2.2), orthographic.

## Look + Lighting

Modular vector geometry, consistent isometric scale, parametric paths, belt index marks, clean coral/navy/cream palette, stable down-right shadows.

## Sound

Silent; no voice, machinery, music, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One package transfer. Exactly four stations; no vehicles, data pulses, perspective convergence, extra packages, cuts, camera motion, text, logos, or post-stop motion.
