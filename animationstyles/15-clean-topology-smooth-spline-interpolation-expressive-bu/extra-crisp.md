# Title

Firefly in the Greenhouse

## Intent

Use an eye-led performance hierarchy and restrained secondary motion to make one gentle capture readable.

## Shot Class

Waist-up character shot, 65 mm equivalent, slow semicircular dolly.

## Subject / Continuity

A stylized young botanist with freckles, short black curls, and a fixed rust apron cups one glowing firefly. Preserve face identity, five-finger anatomy, apron straps, and firefly scale.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): eyes notice the firefly at screen-right. Beat 2 (0.60-1.20): head follows. Beats 3-4 (1.20-2.40): torso turns and hands rise together along one cupping arc. Beats 5-6 (2.40-3.60): palms close gently around the light; fingers retain clean anatomy. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): apron straps lag and settle. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): hands reopen to reveal the perched firefly and a small held smile. Hold through 5.1667 s. Camera arcs continuously 20 degrees and settles by beat 8.

## Spatial Block

Subject rooted at (0,0,0); hands (+0.18,+0.18,−0.02) to (0,+0.28,−0.10). Camera (−0.35,+1.35,−2.0) to (+0.35,+1.35,−2.0), never crossing subject.

## Look + Lighting

Clean subdivision topology, smooth spline animation, controlled facial shapes, soft PBR greenhouse bounce, gentle subsurface skin, warm firefly key growing during the cup.

## Sound

Silent take; no dialogue, music, foley, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One cupping action. No seed, backpack, catch impact, extra insects, hand fusion, identity drift, cuts, zoom, text, logos, or motion after final hold.
