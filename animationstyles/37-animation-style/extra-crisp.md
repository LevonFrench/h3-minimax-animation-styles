# Title

Paper Fern Tea Reveal

## Intent

Frame a tea tin with one tactile hinged-paper bloom whose opening and resolved stillness preserve handcrafted evidence.

## Shot Class

Medium product tabletop, 70 mm equivalent, locked practical camera.

## Subject / Continuity

One unbranded sage-green tea tin remains centered and unchanged. Five folded paper fern leaves hinge from behind it; preserve paper fibers, fold memory, cut edges, wood grain, and contact shadows.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): closed product pose. Beats 2-5 (0.60-3.00): five leaves unfold sequentially left-to-right in stepped poses with slight overlap. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): leaves make one tiny handcrafted settle while tin stays perfectly still. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): complete fern halo holds around hero tin. Hold through 5.1667 s; camera locked.

## Spatial Block

Tin fixed at (0,0,0), 0.18 m tall; leaf hinges within X=±0.24, Y=0.05-0.28 m. Camera fixed at (0,+0.22,−1.35), 70 mm.

## Look + Lighting

Tactile stop-motion, paper fibers, imperfect edges, fold memory, tiny registration shifts, walnut tabletop, practical warm falloff, persistent shadows.

## Sound

Silent; no dialogue, music, paper sounds, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One unfolding action. No readable label, changing tin geometry, leaf duplication, smooth CG interpolation, camera movement, cuts, text, logos, or motion after 4.80 s.
