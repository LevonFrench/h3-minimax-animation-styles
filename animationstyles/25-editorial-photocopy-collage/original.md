# Animation style 25

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 editorial motion-design sequence composed for 864x480, combining scanned paper, torn photographic fragments, marker strokes, halftone texture, masking, photocopier grain, and fast After Effects-style collage transitions. A generic black-and-white photograph of a city cyclist tears into three vertical strips. The strips slide apart to reveal a bright yellow paper field beneath them. Hand-drawn arrows race around the cyclist, a red circle stamps over one wheel, and oversized cut-paper letters assemble into the exact title "KEEP THE CITY MOVING". The frame flips like a physical page, briefly exposing tape, registration marks, and rough edges, then lands on a balanced poster composition.

## Soundscape

Paper tears, tape peels, marker scratches, a rubber-stamp impact, and rapid page flips create a tactile editorial sound bed.

## Music

Chopped drums, upright-bass samples, handclaps, and short brass fragments create a brisk editorial montage rhythm.
