# Research — Rubber-Hose Cartoon Motion

## Actionable vocabulary

- 1920s–30s studio cartoons favored rhythmic black-and-white graphic forms, elastic limbs and bodies, jazz-age timing, and surreal visual play.
- Squash and stretch should preserve apparent volume; the sphere’s pancake, tall oval, ripple, and spin need clean extremes and musical spacing.
- Gate weave and exposure variation should remain subtle post texture, not destabilize the centered action.
- Rubber-hose energy reads through springy arcs, repeated visual accents, and simple high-contrast staging.
