# Title

Telescoping Heron Bridge

## Intent

Demonstrate reusable vector parts and mechanical continuity through three discrete extensions and one clean settle.

## Shot Class

Graphic side elevation, 55 mm equivalent, locked camera.

## Subject / Continuity

A coral vector heron with reusable oval body, hinged neck segments, and flat navy wing symbols stands between two geometric piers. All parts remain connected at visible pivots.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): compact heron holds. Beats 2-4 (0.60-2.40): neck telescopes across the gap in exactly three discrete increments, one per beat. Beats 5-6 (2.40-3.60): head hooks the far pier and body follows along one parabolic swing. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): segments retract with snappy easing. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): heron settles upright on the far side with one restrained elastic overshoot. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Heron starts (−0.34,0,0), ends (+0.34,0,0); neck extends to X=+0.30 m. Camera fixed at (0,+0.20,−1.8), orthographic-like view.

## Look + Lighting

Crisp resolution-independent contours, reusable symbol parts, pivot hinges, flat coral-to-orange gradients, clean shape interpolation. Background gradient brightens subtly at landing.

## Sound

Silent visual take; no dialogue, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One crossing action. No detached parts, freeform morph, robot, multiple jumps, realistic feathers, cuts, camera motion, text, logos, excessive bounce, or endpoint drift.
