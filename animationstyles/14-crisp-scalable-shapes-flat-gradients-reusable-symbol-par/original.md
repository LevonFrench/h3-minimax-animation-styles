# Animation style 14

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 vector animation composed for 864x480, using crisp scalable shapes, flat gradients, reusable symbol parts, motion tweens, pivot-based limbs, shape interpolation, and intentionally snappy easing. An original triangular delivery robot rolls toward a closed gate. Its telescoping arm extends in three mechanically perfect increments, presses the button, and retracts. The gate remains closed. The robot's eye symbol changes from a circle to a narrow rectangle, its entire body compresses vertically, and it springs over the gate through one exaggerated tweened arc before landing with a clean elastic settle.

## Soundscape

Small electric motors whir, interface tones beep, the button clicks, and the robot's spring produces a synthetic boing followed by a neat landing tap.

## Music

Minimal electronic funk with dry drum-machine hits, short bass notes, and bright sampled chords follows the clean vector timing.
