# Research — Vector-tweened symbol animation

## Identification and provenance

This is software-era production vocabulary, not a historical art movement.

## Historically and technically grounded mechanics

Crisp scalable shapes, reusable symbol parts, flat gradients, nested pivots, path interpolation, and controlled easing create clean modular motion.

## Actionable motion, material, and camera vocabulary

- Preserve material evidence, edge behavior, motivated overlap, readable spacing, and a stable endpoint.
- Keep the robot assembled from persistent components with stable pivots; every mechanical action must preserve geometry and hierarchy through the tween.
- Camera vocabulary: gentle centered push-in, 3% scale amplitude, slow ease-out; continuous screen direction; no unmotivated reframing.

## Prompt implications

Name visible causes, not style alone. Preserve the original subject/action, use legible intermediate states and coherent space, and reserve the final tail for a settled hold.
