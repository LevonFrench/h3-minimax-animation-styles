# Animation style 28

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 motion-design animation composed for 864x480, using text-on-path controls, a flowing 3D ribbon, per-character orientation, depth-aware masking, and smooth spline-based movement. A blue ribbon enters from the left, loops around the camera, and crosses the frame in layered curves. The exact phrase "IDEAS TRAVEL FURTHER" appears along its surface, with every letter following the changing direction while remaining upright and readable. The ribbon folds through the center, briefly hiding sections of the phrase behind itself, then straightens into a clean horizontal band. The words detach from the ribbon one at a time, slide into a centered title arrangement, and settle without drifting.

## Soundscape

Soft fabric-like sweeps, subtle passing-air effects, and precise clicks accompany the ribbon folds and final character alignment.

## Music

Flowing synthesizer arpeggios, light hand percussion, and a steady bass note follow the ribbon's curves and finish with a clean rhythmic stop.
