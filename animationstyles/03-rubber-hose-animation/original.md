# Animation style 3

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 monochrome animation composed for 864x480, using elastic rubber-hose limbs, rounded black-and-white forms, looping rhythmic bounce, high-contrast ink shapes, gentle gate weave, and synchronized musical action. An original bean-shaped street sweeper marches beside a trash bin whose lid behaves like a cymbal. The sweeper's knees pump in repeating arcs, both arms stretch around the bin, and the broom bends deeply before snapping straight. On the final beat, the bin compresses through exaggerated squash, launches the rubbish upward, and catches every piece neatly beneath its lid.

## Soundscape

The broom swishes rhythmically, the metal lid clangs, loose cans rattle, and each elastic stretch produces a soft whistle.

## Music

Bouncy mono jazz with honky-tonk piano, muted trumpet, tuba, and woodblock accents synchronizes tightly with each pose and impact.
