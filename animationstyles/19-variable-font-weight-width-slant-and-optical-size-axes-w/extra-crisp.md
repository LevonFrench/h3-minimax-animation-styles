# Title

TIDELINE Axis Passage

## Intent

Animate registered font axes without fake distortion, preserving every counter and the strict left grid.

## Shot Class

Full-frame typographic study, orthographic locked camera.

## Subject / Continuity

Exact uppercase word “TIDELINE,” black on warm white, set on a fixed left alignment. Glyph topology, spelling, baseline, and counters remain invariant.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): regular master holds. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): wght increases smoothly from 400 to 760 while counters stay open. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): wdth narrows from 100 to 78 without changing left edge. Beat 6 (3.00-3.60): slnt moves gently to −8 degrees. Beat 7 (3.60-4.20): opsz resolves to a display cut, preserving topology. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): all axes settle at the final registered instance. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Left edge fixed at X=−0.42 m; baseline Y=0; changing advance width extends only toward screen-right. Camera fixed at (0,0,−1.5).

## Look + Lighting

Pristine variable-font interpolation, strict grid, responsive frame, flat black/warm-white palette. Even graphic illumination, no shadows.

## Sound

Silent take; no voice, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

Exact text only: TIDELINE. No raster warp, envelope distortion, broken counters, simultaneous axis extremes, extra glyphs, misspelling, cuts, camera motion, logos, or endpoint drift.
