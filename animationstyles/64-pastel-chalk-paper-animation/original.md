# Animation style 64

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 animation composed for 864x480, using soft pastel and chalk on textured paper with powdery edges, smudged color blending, visible dust, and hand-worked shading. A single lavender sphere remains perfectly centered on a muted paper field at constant size with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere slowly brightens from one side as if a pastel highlight is rubbed into place, then the shadow side deepens with finger-smudged blending. Fine pastel dust lifts from the contour, swirls gently around the sphere, and settles back into the paper while the sphere returns to its original balance of light and shadow.

## Soundscape

Powdery rubbing sounds, soft dust sweeps, and gentle fingertip smudges give the animation a tactile handmade quality.

## Music

A soft blend of flute, felted piano, and brushed percussion follows the gradual tonal change.
