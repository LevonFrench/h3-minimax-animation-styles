# Research — Pastel and chalk animation on textured paper

## Provenance and identification

The source describes dry pastel/chalk marks animated as if rubbed and smudged frame by frame. The Met explains pastel as dry pigment and binder interacting strongly with a textured support; Disney and the Academy ground the frame-by-frame drawing process. None of these sources establishes “pastel sphere animation” as a discrete historical school.

## Visual mechanics

- Paper tooth should catch granular pigment and break smooth areas into tactile marks.
- Build the highlight through progressive rubbing and deepen the shadow through directional smudging.
- Dust lifts from the contour, makes one gentle circulation, and returns to the paper.
- Preserve a soft edge while maintaining the sphere's legible circular boundary.

## Prompt implications

Specify pigment behavior and tool action. Avoid turning dust into glitter or volumetric smoke, and restore the original light-shadow balance.
