# Animation style 31

## Prompt

A5.00-second 16:9 digital-out-of-home test composed for 864x480, showing a large corner-mounted urban LED billboard from the intended street viewing position. The screen displays a photoreal animated orange cube inside a deep virtual room. Forced perspective makes the room appear to extend physically into the building. The cube rolls toward the corner seam, strikes it, and breaks into smaller cubes that appear to spill beyond the screen edge toward the street. They pause in apparent open space, reverse direction, and snap back into the billboard. The pieces assemble into the exact text "STEP INSIDE" while the illusionary room returns to a flat graphic background.

## Soundscape

City traffic and distant pedestrians surround deep virtual impacts, small tumbling cube sounds, and one sharp reverse suction as the pieces retum to the display.

## Music

Low electronic percussion and short spatial synthesizer sweeps accent the forced-perspective impacts and final title assembly.
