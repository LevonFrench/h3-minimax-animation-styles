# Title

Xerox Berry Dash

## Intent

Use one intentional smear drawing and two damped wobbles to showcase rough transferred contours without generic video noise.

## Shot Class

Medium side view, 55 mm equivalent, locked camera.

## Subject / Continuity

One red berry with two green leaves and rough xerographic pencil contour moves across a flat cream cel field. Preserve leaf count, flat paint, copied-line texture, and apparent volume.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): berry anticipates at left. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): it accelerates right with variable copied contour. Beat 4 (1.80-2.40): one single-frame horizontal smear at peak speed. Beat 5 (2.40-3.00): clean recovery drawing at target. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): exactly two decreasing lateral wobbles. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): berry holds cleanly through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Berry (−0.42,0,0) to (+0.30,0,0), fixed average Y; smear confined to X=−0.05 to +0.30. Camera fixed at (0,+0.15,−1.6).

## Look + Lighting

Rough transferred pencil contour, restrained copied-line density shifts, flat cel paint, spontaneous dry line, stable cream field.

## Sound

Silent; no dash sound, voice, music, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One dash. Exactly one smear and two wobbles; no generic video noise, extra fruit, camera motion, cuts, text, logos, or motion after hold.
