# Research — Xerographic Cel Line

## Actionable vocabulary

- Xerography transferred animation drawings directly to cels, preserving spontaneous pencil texture while removing hand-inking. Expect rough, variable contours rather than polished ink lines.
- Flatter painted color and a copied-line density shift can evoke successive Xerox transfers; keep that variation restrained enough to preserve form.
- A one-frame horizontal smear is an intentional speed drawing, followed by a clean recovery and two damped wobbles.
- Distinguish xerographic roughness from generic video noise: texture belongs to the copied contour.
