# Animation style 45

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 clay stop-motion animation composed for 864x480, using softly sculpted plasticine, practical miniature lighting, visible thumbprints, stepped pose increments, and subtle registration jitter. An original clay swimmer stands at the end of a miniature diving board above blue foil water. The swimmer approaches the edge, looks down, and pulls back through a visible hesitation. One foot retreats, the shoulders rise, and the replacement mouth shifts from confidence to concern. After a brief held pose, the swimmer inhales, swings both arms backward, bends deeply through a strong anticipation, and drives forward. The board bends before releasing. The swimmer leaves frame in a clean diving arc while the board continues vibrating after the departure.

## Soundscape

Clay feet stick lightly to the board, the miniature structure creaks, and the diver takes one nervous breath. The board releases with a sharp spring sound.

## Music

Plucked strings and muted woodblocks pause during the hesitation, then accelerate into the jump.
