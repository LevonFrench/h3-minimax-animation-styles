# Title

Clay Kite Leap

## Intent

Make hesitation, board deflection, release, and a forward-downward parabola read as one tactile jump.

## Shot Class

Medium-wide miniature side view, 55 mm equivalent, locked camera.

## Subject / Continuity

A plasticine child in a fixed orange swimsuit stands on a small blue springboard above a dark miniature pool. Preserve fingerprints, limb geometry, board hinge, mouth plates, shadows, and water plane.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): hesitant pose, weight back and gaze fixed down. Beat 2 (0.60-1.20): one foot retreats, shoulders lift. Beats 3-4 (1.20-2.40): character commits forward as board bends visibly. Beat 5 (2.40-3.00): board releases. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): character follows one clean forward-downward parabola, limbs held coherent. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): fingertips touch water and freeze before splash. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Character (+0.00,+0.35,0) to (+0.42,0,0), apex (+0.20,+0.48,0); board at X=−0.12 to +0.10. Camera fixed at (0,+0.25,−1.8).

## Look + Lighting

Soft plasticine, thumbprints, stepped poses, replacement mouth only at hesitation extreme, practical miniature shadows, warm pool light.

## Sound

Silent; no splash, dialogue, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One jump. No dive arc ambiguity, extra people, limb warping, board geometry changes, cuts, camera motion, text, logos, or splash after the hold.
