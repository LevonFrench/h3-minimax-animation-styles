# Research — Soft Pastel Material Behavior

## Actionable vocabulary

- Pastel consists of finely ground pigment with very little binder; toothy paper grips the powder. Broad-side strokes reveal paper texture, while pressure creates saturated marks.
- Finger or stump blending produces soft transitions; loose powder and diffuse light create a velvety matte bloom.
- Animate a highlight as newly rubbed pigment and a shadow as layered/smudged strokes. Dust should lift from the contour and settle onto the paper, not behave like sparks.
- Preserve powdery edges and avoid glossy highlights or wet-paint flow.
