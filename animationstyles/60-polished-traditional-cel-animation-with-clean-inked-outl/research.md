# Research — Polished traditional cel animation

## Provenance and identification

Cel animation is a historical production technique in which inked/painted elements are photographed frame by frame; the Academy guide presents cel animation as the familiar form alongside alternative media. Disney's hand-drawn process distinguishes rough animation, cleanup, in-betweens, and consistent on-model drawing. The source's clean outlines, opaque color, airbrushed background, and moving painted highlight are compatible visual cues, though “on ones” specifies exposure timing rather than the cel material itself.

## Visual mechanics

- Keep ink contour weight clean and stable while squash/stretch remains modest.
- Coordinate vertical bounce, contact squash, and highlight rotation as one coherent form.
- Use opaque red fill and a painted specular accent rather than photoreal ray-traced reflection.
- Sparkles orbit on a clean circular path and disappear before restoration.

## Prompt implications

Count one bounce and one spin, protect the fixed screen center, and ensure the final pose restores both geometry and painted shading.
