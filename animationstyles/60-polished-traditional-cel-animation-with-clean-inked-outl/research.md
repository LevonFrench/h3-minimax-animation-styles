# Research — Polished Traditional Cel Finish

## Actionable vocabulary

- Cel animation separates moving painted elements from reusable backgrounds. Clean-up lines retain the animator’s intent while keeping the form on-model.
- Opaque flat paint, controlled airbrushed background, consistent painted highlight, and smooth animation on ones create a polished feature-era finish.
- The bounce should use anticipation, small squash/stretch, clean arcs, and a stable endpoint; orbiting sparkles remain a separate effects layer.
- Keep apparent volume and painted shading consistent across the spin.
