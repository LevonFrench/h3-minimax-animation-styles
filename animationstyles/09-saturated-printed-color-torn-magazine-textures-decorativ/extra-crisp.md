# Title

Postcard Volcano Bouquet

## Intent

Make one transformation read as physical replacement animation, preserving torn edges, print direction, and rostrum shadows.

## Shot Class

Top-down medium composition, 55 mm equivalent, slow 8 cm rostrum push.

## Subject / Continuity

A triangular volcano cut from a faded travel postcard sits on cobalt paper. Separate torn-paper smoke puffs are stacked inside its crater; all elements retain visible fibers, halftone, tape edges, and consistent cast shadows.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): volcano rests. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): one smoke stack rises by discrete paper substitutions. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): successive puffs hinge outward, revealing saturated printed flower petals. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): the replacements open into one bouquet without dissolves or scale warps. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): a perforated postage-stamp sun slides behind the bouquet and stops. Hold through 5.1667 s. Camera pushes continuously downward and settles by 4.80.

## Spatial Block

Volcano centered at (0,0,0); bouquet grows Y=+0.10 to +0.34 m in paper plane; sun ends at (+0.16,+0.28,+0.01). Camera (0,+0.75,0) to (0,+0.67,0), perpendicular.

## Look + Lighting

Saturated printed color, torn magazine fiber, mismatched halftone directions, tape edges, postage perforation, shallow rostrum parallax. Warm side light strengthens slightly as petals open while shadow direction stays fixed.

## Sound

Silent take; no dialogue, music, paper foley, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One smoke-to-bouquet replacement action. No wings, flying subject, liquid morph, smooth CG scaling, extra props, cuts, rotation of paper grain, changing shadows, text, logos, or endpoint drift.
