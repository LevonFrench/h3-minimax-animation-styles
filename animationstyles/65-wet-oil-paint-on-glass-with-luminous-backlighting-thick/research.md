# Research — Paint-on-Glass Animation

## Actionable vocabulary

- Paint-on-glass is an under-camera, frame-by-frame technique in which wet pigment is pushed, wiped, blended, and repainted between exposures.
- Caroline Leaf’s work demonstrates the technique’s fluid transformations and handmade continuity. Visible brush drag, finger wipe, and pigment pooling are more authentic than digital liquid simulation.
- Backlighting should glow through thin paint and become dense where pigment accumulates. Edges can soften by wiping, then be repainted.
- Preserve one centered form and one white painted ribbon to keep the action readable.
