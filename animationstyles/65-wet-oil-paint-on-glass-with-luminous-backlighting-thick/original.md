# Animation style 65

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 animation composed for 864x480, using wet oil paint on glass with luminous backlighting, thick pigment, visible brush pressure, and fluid frame-by-frame repainting. A single deep blue sphere remains perfectly centered at a constant scale with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere's surface appears to swirl internally, with wet brush strokes circulating around a bright central highlight. The edges soften and bleed outward slightly, then are repainted back to a crisp circular boundary. A ribbon of white paint rotates around the sphere like a ring of light and disappears into the original highlight position by the end.

## Soundscape

Wet brush drags, soft liquid swells, and low bubbling textures match the moving pigment.

## Music

Slow bowed strings, glassy harmonics, and a distant low drone give the sphere a luminous painterly atmosphere.
