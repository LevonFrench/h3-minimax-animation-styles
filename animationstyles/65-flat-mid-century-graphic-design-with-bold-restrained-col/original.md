# Animation style 65

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 animation composed for 864x480, using flat mid-century graphic design with bold restrained color blocks, crisp edges, sparse linework, and selective limited movement. A single teal sphere remains perfectly centered on a cream field with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere becomes a flat circular disc, then expands into concentric rings, then collapses back to a clean filled circle. its shadow slides one step left, then one step right, while the sphere itself remains centered. Small geometric rectangles appear around it in symmetrical balance, then withdraw, restoring the original minimalist layout.

## Soundscape

Clean clicks, soft panel slides, and one restrained tonal pulse support the economical graphic transformations.
