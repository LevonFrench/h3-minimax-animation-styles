# Animation style 2

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 animation composed for 864x480, using ornate black silhouette cutouts, articulated paper joints, decorative profiles, theatrical staging, and layered glass-plane parallax. A long-legged traveler crosses a moonlit bridge carrying a lantern. His shoulders, elbows, knees, and ankles rotate through clearly stepped cut-paper poses. A patterned shadow-bird rises from beneath the bridge, circles him in profile, and lands on the lantern. The traveler anticipates with a backward lean, bows carefully, and. continues walking while foreground reeds and distant hills slide at different speeds.

## Soundscape

Paper joints click softly, the bridge gives a dry wooden creak, the lantern handle taps, and distant nighttime wind moves through reeds.

## Music

Sparse chamber accompaniment with plucked harp, bowed cello, and small hand drum follows the deliberate puppet-like movement. @STVie = @SuBJECT @ ACTION
