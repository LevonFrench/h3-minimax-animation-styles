# Title

The Moth Opens the Observatory

## Intent

Stage an ornate silhouette mechanism whose readable profile and negative space carry the action.

## Shot Class

Wide theatrical tableau, 65 mm equivalent, locked rostrum camera, frontal proscenium view.

## Subject / Continuity

A jointed black-paper moth with pin-hinged wings stands before a circular observatory shutter. Moth, shutter, fern foreground, and translucent hill background remain distinct physical planes.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): moth holds wings closed. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): both wings articulate outward at visible paper hinges. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): wing tips engage two lace-cut levers, rotating the shutter as one continuous action. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): the aperture opens to reveal an amber moon. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): wings lock into a symmetrical crown around it. Hold the endpoint through 5.1667 s; camera remains locked throughout.

## Spatial Block

Moth centered at (0,+0.14,0), wings spanning 0.34 m; shutter at (0,+0.15,+0.03). Camera fixed at (0,+0.18,-1.25), level and frontal. Foreground fern at Z=-0.06, hills at Z=+0.08.

## Look + Lighting

Flat black cut paper, crisp profiles, lace-like perforations, slight fiber edges, translucent amber and muted teal backlight, delicate glass-plane parallax only from separated shadows. Backlight warms once as the moon is exposed.

## Sound

Silent visual take; no dialogue, music, effects, or generated soundtrack.

## Limits

One action, one locked shot. No interior facial detail, free-floating parts, rubber limbs, live-action textures, gradients on black paper, cuts, zooms, text, logos, or endpoint motion.
