# Title

Harbor Signal Explainer

## Intent

Reveal one route through shallow vector depth while a null-parented camera performs a restrained push and square-on return.

## Shot Class

Wide 2.5D explainer composition, 45 mm equivalent, continuous push-return camera.

## Subject / Continuity

A coral ferry icon, cream pier card, navy beacon, distant warehouses, and two cloud cards occupy fixed separated Z planes. No text.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): frontal harbor tableau. Beats 2-4 (0.60-2.40): camera pushes 0.22 m as one white route line emerges from the ferry toward the beacon. Beats 5-6 (2.40-3.60): route line rounds the pier and reaches the beacon; card edges reveal controlled parallax. Beats 7-8 (3.60-4.80): camera returns square-on while the beacon ring expands once and stops. Hold through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Ferry Z=0, pier +0.04 m, beacon +0.08 m, buildings +0.16 m, clouds +0.22 m. Camera (0,+0.20,−1.8) to (0,+0.20,−1.58) and back to start.

## Look + Lighting

Layered vector artwork, restrained parallax, soft cast shadows, visible card edges, clean coral/navy/cream palette. Beacon adds one warm halo at route completion.

## Sound

Silent; no voiceover, dialogue, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One route reveal and one continuous camera path. No person, shelter, signs, labels, cuts, orbit, deep perspective, logos, or endpoint motion.
