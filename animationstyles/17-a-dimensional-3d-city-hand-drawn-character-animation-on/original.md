# Animation style 17

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 contemporary hybrid animation composed for 864x480, combining a dimensional 3D city, hand-drawn character animation on twos, variable frame rates, inked contours, halftone shading, chromatic misregistration, painted effects, stepped poses, and brief graphic impact frames. An original roller skater races down a curved rooftop ramp. The environment moves smoothly at full frame rate while the skater holds bold graphic poses with intentionally stepped spacing. She launches across an alley, rotates through a hand-drawn smear, and passes through a ‘one-frame panel of abstract color and oversized motion lines. She lands in the 3D space, skids sideways, and leaves a painted brushstroke trail that remains suspended behind her,

## Soundscape

Skate wheels rattle, wind rushes between buildings, the ramp vibrates, and the landing combines a wheel impact, paper slap, and short spray-paint hiss.

## Music

Punchy hip-hop drums, chopped brass, elastic synth bass, and turntable scratches shift rhythmically with the changing frame rates.
