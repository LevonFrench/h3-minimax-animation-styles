# Research — Premium Product Reveal

## Actionable vocabulary

- Product visualization depends on controlled specular highlights, clean reflections, exact silhouettes, and restrained camera or turntable motion.
- A polished black sphere reads through edge light, reflected environment bands, a grounded contact reflection, and subtle surface microdetail.
- Keep the object centered and geometrically stable; let one highlight sweep and one halo bloom carry the action.
- Use condensation-like sparkle only as a brief material accent, not a particle storm.
