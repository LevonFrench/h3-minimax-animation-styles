# Animation style 75

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 stylized CG animation composed for 864x480, using clean topology, soft physically based lighting, polished materials, smooth spline motion, and cinematic shading. A single glossy teal sphere remains: perfectly centered on a softly lit studio background at a constant size with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere rotates through exactly one smooth half-turn while a soft highlight glides across its surface. The sphere then compresses subtly as if made from a resilient gel, rebounds, and releases a ring of tiny floating light particles that orbit once and dissolve. The final frame restores the original front-facing shading and stillness.
