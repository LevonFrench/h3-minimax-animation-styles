# Research — Contemporary Stylized PBR Motion

## Actionable vocabulary

- PBR materials separate diffuse, specular, roughness, clear coat, and transmission; a glossy teal gel needs coherent highlight travel and energy-conserving reflections.
- Clean topology keeps squash deformation smooth and prevents pinching. Apparent volume should remain stable during compression and rebound.
- Spline motion benefits from readable ease-in/out and a stable locked camera. The highlight must correspond to fixed studio lights, not slide independently.
- Orbiting particles are a secondary accent; use a small deterministic ring and let them fade before the endpoint.
