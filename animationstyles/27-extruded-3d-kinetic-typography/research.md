# Research: extruded 3D kinetic typography

## Identification and provenance

This is a hybrid of kinetic typography, 3D title design, and cinematic product-style rendering. Adobe identifies text as resolution-independent vector layers that can be animated per character and placed in 3D; Autodesk documents focus-distance control for rendered depth of field. These sources support the mechanics, not a named historical movement.

## Visual and motion mechanics

- Deep extrusion needs readable front faces, consistent bevels, and long sidewalls; repeat the same “FORWARD” geometry at measured depth intervals.
- A centered vanishing corridor and rhythmic rotational offsets create parallax without sacrificing the word.
- Use physically coherent key, fill, shadow direction, and focus falloff. The final long shadow should agree with the light direction.
- The camera crossing the final “O” is a motivated portal cut, after which a single title rotates upright, overshoots, and seats on an invisible baseline.

## Prompt implications

Prioritize one continuous axial push rather than several unrelated camera moves. Keep front faces bright enough to read during travel; shallow focus may soften distant copies but not the hero title. Reserve the clean open field and shadow for the final reveal.
