# Animation style 58

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 monochrome animation composed for 864x480, using high-contrast black-and-white rubber-hose cartoon styling, elastic motion, soft gate weave, and simple graphic staging. A single white sphere with a black outline remains perfectly centered at constant size against a black background, with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere compresses into a pancake, rebounds into a tall stretched oval, ripples like rubber, and spins once in place while maintaining its centered position. Small rhythmic motion lines pop around it on each beat, then vanish as the sphere settles back into its original round form.

## Soundscape

Rubbery boings, soft impact puffs, and tiny whistle-like stretch sounds sync with the sphere's exaggerated elasticity.

## Music

Bouncy mono jazz with piano, tuba, muted trumpet, and woodblock accents lands tightly on each stretch and squash.
