# Animation style 14

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 early computer-animation test composed for 864x480, using black space, luminous vector grids, low-polygon geometry, visible faceting, Gouraud-shaded surfaces, primitive particle trails, and mathematically smooth camera movement. A geometric bird assembles from floating triangles above a glowing grid plane. Its wings rotate through rigid hierarchical joints before gradually gaining smoother interpolation. It accelerates into a wireframe tunnel while the camera follows directly behind. The bird rolls ninety degrees, sheds a trail of polygon fragments, and recombines into a reflective chrome sphere at the tunnel's vanishing point.

## Soundscape

Synthetic servo movements, digital chirps, filtered rushing noise, and crystalline polygon impacts accompany the transformation.

## Music

Arpeggiated analog synthesizers, electronic toms, and a slowly rising digital pad create an optimistic early-computer aesthetic.
