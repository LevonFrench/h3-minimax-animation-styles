Use rigid joint rotations and visible facets first, then introduce smooth interpolation as the bird accelerates; keep the grid vanishing point and camera roll exact.

**SHOT CLASS:** chase camera, 35 mm equivalent; smooth centered spline through tunnel. One physically continuous shot; no unmotivated reframing, zoom, orbit, or generic camera drift.

**SUBJECT AND PRIMARY ACTION:** using black space, luminous vector grids, low-polygon geometry, visible faceting, Gouraud-shaded surfaces, primitive particle trails, and mathematically smooth camera movement. The bird rolls ninety degrees, sheds a trail of polygon fragments, and recombines into a reflective chrome sphere at the tunnel's vanishing point.

**LIGHT AND CONTINUITY:** One coherent lighting progression, stable palette, stable subject design, and persistent material properties. Preserve silhouettes, joint logic, contact shadows, line or grain behavior, and exact title spelling. Transformations must pass through visible intermediate shapes; no cross-dissolves, spontaneous duplicate subjects, broken anatomy, texture swimming, random flicker, or late camera movement.
