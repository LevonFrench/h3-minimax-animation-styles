# Animation style 71

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 stop-motion animation composed for 864x480, using a handcrafted miniature set, tactile materials, soft practical lighting, stepped pose increments, and subtle registration jitter. A single fabric-covered sphere remains perfectly centered on a small pedestal at a constant scale with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere tilts slightly left, then right, as if testing balance, then unfolds along a hidden seam into four padded segments before closing again. Its stitched surface catches the light differently as it rotates one quarter turn and then returns to its exact original front-facing orientation.

## Soundscape

Tiny mechanical clicks, fabric rustles, and miniature pedestal creaks define the stop-motion tactile movement.
