# Research: clay stop-motion anticipation exercise—hammer

## Identification and provenance

This is plasticine stop-motion character animation using hesitation, pose-to-pose staging, exaggerated anticipation, acceleration, impact, and follow-through. Aardman sources support visible modeling-clay craft; Disney’s process names anticipation, timing, and follow-through as core principles. The mechanic-and-hammer scenario is bespoke.

## Visual and motion mechanics

- Doubt is communicated with the half-raise, foot adjustment, target study, lowered hammer, and held pose.
- The backward weight shift and hammer arc load stored energy; one accented held extreme clarifies silhouette.
- Downstroke spacing widens dramatically into one clean contact with the peg.
- Torso follow-through passes the target after the hammer, while the peg sinks rather than bounces.

## Prompt implications

Maintain hammer scale and peg position; use a stable miniature bench and readable side three-quarter silhouette. Do not add repeated strikes. The final held state is post-impact follow-through.
