# Research — Clay Character Weight and Strike

## Actionable vocabulary

- Stop-motion character acting benefits from discrete pose changes and purposeful stillness; the anticipatory hold makes the strike feel forceful.
- Weight transfer should move feet, hips, torso, arms, and hammer in a readable chain. The hammer follows a curved arc, accelerates into contact, and continues through follow-through.
- Keep the peg, bench, hammer head, and hands geometrically stable. A single sunk peg and brief bench rattle communicate impact.
- Practical-light continuity and controlled fingerprints preserve handcrafted credibility.
