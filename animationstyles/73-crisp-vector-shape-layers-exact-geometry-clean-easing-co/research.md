# Research — Vector shape-layer motion design

## Provenance and identification

This is modern vector motion design built from geometric paths and keyframed transforms. Adobe documents shape layers as vector objects and its Graph Editor documentation distinguishes value and speed graphs, eased velocity, motion-path spacing, and Bezier influence. Those sources support construction and timing mechanics, not a named “cobalt sphere” style.

## Visual mechanics

- Keep one exact vector center and mathematically concentric paths.
- Use a small overshoot/undershoot scale phrase with a controlled speed curve.
- Counterrotate concentric strokes around the same anchor.
- Reveal guide lines and anchor points briefly as construction information, then remove them.

## Prompt implications

Specify scale limits and rotation directions; keep motion blur controlled enough that exact geometry remains readable.
