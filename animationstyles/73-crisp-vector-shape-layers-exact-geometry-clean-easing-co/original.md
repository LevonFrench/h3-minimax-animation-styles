# Animation style 73

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 motion-design animation composed for 864x480, using crisp vector shape layers, exact geometry, clean easing, controlled motion blur, and flat digital color. A single cobalt sphere remains perfectly centered on an off-white field at constant size with no camera movement and no cuts. The sphere scales up slightly with soft overshoot, then down slightly, then splits into concentric circular strokes that rotate in opposite directions around the same center. Thin guide lines and small anchor markers appear briefly as if demonstrating the construction, then vanish as the concentric forms collapse back into the original solid sphere.

## Soundscape

Interface clicks, soft swishes, and restrained elastic pops match the vector scaling and circular rotations.

## Music

Minimal electronic percussion and plucked synth tones give the motion a clean modern design rhythm.
