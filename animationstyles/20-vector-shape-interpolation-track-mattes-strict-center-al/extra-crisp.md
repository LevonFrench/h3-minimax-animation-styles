# Title

Acorn to Lighthouse

## Intent

Transform one center-locked vector mass through readable silhouettes while preserving path topology and negative-space logic.

## Shot Class

Centered graphic close-up, orthographic locked camera.

## Subject / Continuity

One ochre acorn silhouette on midnight blue; the same matched path points and single central mass become a lighthouse. No detached pieces.

## Timed Action + Camera

100 BPM. Beat 1 (0.00-0.60): acorn holds. Beats 2-3 (0.60-1.80): cap flattens and body lengthens through clear intermediate silhouettes. Beats 4-5 (1.80-3.00): negative space exchanges inward to form two windows while outer topology remains intact. Beats 6-7 (3.00-4.20): tip becomes a lantern roof with one restrained elastic ease. Beat 8 (4.20-4.80): palette hands off from ochre to coral and lighthouse holds. Maintain exact endpoint through 5.1667 s.

## Spatial Block

Central mass remains centered at (0,0,0), maximum 0.34 m tall and 0.20 m wide. Camera fixed at (0,0,−1.4).

## Look + Lighting

Crisp vector silhouette, matched path topology, track-matte negative space, brief readable stages, flat palette. Uniform graphic light.

## Sound

Silent; no voice, music, effects, or soundtrack.

## Limits

One interpolation action. No dissolve, particles, topology collapse, detached shapes, camera motion, text, logos, extra icons, or motion after 4.80 s.
