# Animation style 11

## Prompt

A 5.00-second 16:9 paint-on-glass animation composed for 864x480, using wet oil pigment, visible brush pressure, luminous backlighting, smeared edges, direct frame-by-frame repainting, and continuous transformation. A small red boat crosses a dark blue painted sea. The waves rise through broad brush strokes and curl around the hull. The boat's sail widens, softens, and becomes the fin of a great painted fish. The hull dissolves into its body as the sea transforms into the night sky. The fish swims upward between wet stars, leaving streaks of disturbed pigment behind it.

## Soundscape

Water laps softly, wooden boards creak, wet brushes drag across glass, and the transformed fish produces one deep underwater breath.

## Music

Slow bowed strings, glass harmonics, and a distant wooden flute sustain through the fluid metamorphosis. @STVie QP SUBJECT @ ACTION
