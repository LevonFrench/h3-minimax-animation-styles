# Research: graphic match-cut transformation

## Identification and provenance

This is a commercial motion-design hybrid built around graphic match cuts: unrelated objects share center, circular contour, direction, and motion so cuts read as transformations. Time remapping and speed control shape the acceleration between matches. Adobe’s speed-graph documentation connects frame-dot spacing to visible speed; its vector-path documentation supports mask- and shape-based transitions. “Center-locked match-cut motion design” is descriptive rather than a formal school.

## Visual and motion mechanics

- Maintain one exact screen-space center and clockwise rotation across cup rim, sun, wheel, record, and final circle.
- Match silhouette, scale, tangent speed, and highlight direction at every cut.
- Use speed ramps to hide cuts at peak motion, then allow one readable beat for each new object.
- Transform the record highlight into the white field, preserving direction and causal continuity.

## Prompt implications

Treat the circular motif as the dominant subject. Avoid independent scene resets. A locked camera makes the central match geometry unmistakable; environmental changes happen inside the centered circle.
